## 1. Why this version exists

V261 closed Track C, the last data-blocked *offline* lane. The refutation-pattern
taxonomy in the V241–V258 retrospective is unambiguous:

- **R2 (signal below eval resolution)** accounts for 5 of 13 dead ends (~40%).
- **R3 (calendar-bound N)** is the *meta-cause* — the V247 ruler showed the `recent`
  regime has **4 independent 90d windows** in the entire 2020→2026 span, MDE
  ≈ **$727–$1,043** (2·SE). Any effect below that floor is structurally
  unfalsifiable, no matter how many mechanisms we try.
- V249 declared the phase transition on exactly this basis. The two declared resume
  paths were **(1) live-paper accrual to recent-N ≥ 20** (≈4 years of calendar, now
  running on the V253 daemon) and **(2) a new data source that changes regime
  structure — explicitly naming "intraday OHLCV freeze"** as the entry-side reopener.

**This version is resume path (2).** It is the last major untried axis and the only
one that does not require waiting on the calendar.

## 2. Alpha thesis (pre-declared)

> At intraday resolution, per-name regime structure decouples from the macro
> regime cycle. If that decoupling is real, independent observation windows
> accumulate ~24× faster at 1h (and ~288× at 5m) than the daily bar permits,
> which would:
>
> 1. **Reopen R2-refuted signals for a genuine retest** — a signal that was real
>    but below the daily eval's resolution becomes measurable.
> 2. **Reveal microstructure signals invisible at daily granularity** — funding
>    twist (intra-8h drift), basis mean-reversion, order-flow imbalance.
> 3. **Fix the V247 ruler problem structurally**, by manufacturing training-N per
>    unit calendar time rather than waiting for it.

### 2a. The load-bearing assumption — stated explicitly, because it is the whole bet

**24× more *bars* is NOT 24× more *independent windows*.** The V249 constraint is a
statement about independent **90-day regime windows** (26 in the span, fully tiled).
Sampling the same 90 days at 1h gives 2,160 bars instead of 90 — that shrinks the
*within-window* standard error, but it does **not** create new 90-day windows.

The N-multiplication claim only holds if intraday regime structure is **genuinely
orthogonal** to macro-daily regime — i.e. if a 3.75-day window (90 bars at 1h) is a
legitimately independent regime observation rather than 1/24th of a daily-regime
window re-labelled. If intraday regime is just the daily regime resampled, the
windows share information, effective N does not grow, and V262 reduces to "the same
saturated composite measured with a finer ruler on the same 26 draws."

**This is why falsifier F4 below is not a nice-to-have — it is the gate on the
entire thesis.** It is pre-declared as a hard REFUTE condition, and it should be run
*first* in V262-2, before any PnL grid, as the Phase-0 separator proof (standing rule
from V234).

silently would inject a fabricated 3.3-day price jump into every trailing-window

## 6. Falsifiers for V262-2 (PRE-REGISTERED — all four are hard REFUTE conditions)

Run in this order; **F4 is the Phase-0 separator proof and runs first.**

**F4 — regime independence (the gate on the thesis).**
Compute per-name hourly regime labels and correlate against the macro-day regime
label. If **correlation > 0.7**, intraday regime is the daily regime with more
sampling — no new information, effective N does not grow → **REFUTED**, stop before
any grid. *(This is the cheap probe that would have saved V234's entire burn.)*

**F1 — pooled median PnL ≤ $0** on the 1h walk-forward grid → **REFUTED**.

**F2 — MWU p ≥ 0.05** on winners vs losers split by entry-composite z → the
mechanism does not hold at intraday resolution → **REFUTED**.
*(Same test that killed V261 at p=0.942.)*

**F3 — annualized net < 15%**, fee-adjusted at intraday trade frequency → alpha is
eaten by microstructure cost → **REFUTED**. Fee model must be pre-declared; the
V255.B precedent is decisive here (gross 36.4% annualized was real alpha and still
died at −$5.95 median net once a 2-leg 20bps friction was applied over a 3-day hold).
At 1h the trade count rises by construction, so this is the **most likely** killer.

## 7. Known risks, stated up front

| Risk | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| **Transaction-cost erosion** | Highest-probability failure. More bars ⇒ more trades ⇒ friction scales linearly while edge does not. F3 exists for this. |
| **Microstructure noise** | 1h bars carry bid-ask bounce and thin-book artifacts that daily bars average away. |
| **Overfit risk on tighter bars** | 24× the bars means 24× the opportunities to fit noise. The pre-declaration in §5 is the only defence. |
| **Regime label validity** | `walk_forward_freeze.py:regime_label` is defined on daily bars. An intraday regime labeller is **new code that does not exist yet** and is itself a V262-2 deliverable with its own correctness risk. |
| **Effective-N illusion** | §2a. If F4 fails, everything else is moot. |
