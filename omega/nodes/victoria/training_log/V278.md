## §2 — Hypothesis

> `dxy_signal` and `yield_curve` consume macro data from the replayed bar's future in
> every frozen run. Fencing them under `OMEGA_FROZEN_CACHE` — **exactly as `vix_signal`
> and `spy_signal` already are** — makes the frozen path causally honest.

V240.B measured both as **near-inert** contributors, so the *predicted* effect is small.
That prediction is the thing being tested, not a reason to skip the test: V273's own
wording is that four years of lookahead *"were structurally available and measured to
contribute little, which is a finding about effect size, not about correctness."*

**Falsifier:** if fencing moves any family beyond the determinism floor, the standing
baseline is contaminated by macro lookahead and must be re-derived on the full grid.
That is a finding, and it **stops** here — V278 will not attempt the re-derivation.
