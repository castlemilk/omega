## 0. What this version is, and what it explicitly is NOT

V271 stopped at R5 with a specific, narrow finding:

> `FundingRegimeClassifier.classify_span` (`omega/nodes/funding_carry/regime.py`)
> standardizes the market funding index over the **full span**. Every date's
> regime label therefore depends on the mean/std of the entire series,
> including dates in that date's future. V255.B/C/D exclude `near_zero`, so
> **21.5% of dates flip trade/no-trade** when the label is recomputed causally
> (pre-registered bar was 5%), and the filter gates **53.5%** of
> level-passing candidate symbol-days.

That does **not** invalidate the retrospective V255.C/D **ADOPT** verdict —
full-span labelling is a legitimate descriptive classification inside a
*scorer*. It invalidates running that rule **forward**.

V272 is:

- **IS** a bounded causal re-specification of exactly one function
  (`_standardize` → expanding-window), scored side-by-side against the
  frozen ledger and against a no-filter control.
- **IS NOT** a strategy redesign. `basis_hedge.py`, `hold_scaled.py` and
  `strategy.py` are not touched. The entry threshold, hold, sizing, fee model
  and basis source are all held at V255.C/D values.
- **IS NOT** a search. Exactly two variants are scored. No per-symbol slice,
  no regime-conditional cell, no third variant. If the A-vs-B result is
  surprising, that is a V272-2 with its own pre-registration.

## 1. Pre-registered question

> Does the funding-carry regime filter carry real signal that survives causal
> (expanding-window) labelling, or was its edge a full-span standardization
> artefact?

## 2. The two variants

Both are scored against the **frozen V255.C ledger** with
`--basis-source frozen` (V255.D real perp-mark + spot-index basis), identical
`HoldScaledParams()` defaults (`level_thresh=1e-4`, `hold_days=7`,
`base=cap=$10k`, `funding_ref_bps=5`, `fee_bps_per_side=5`, `n_legs=2`).

**Variant A — causal re-specification.**
`FundingRegimeClassifier.classify_span_causal(dates, market_index)`:
identical to `classify_span` except the two `_standardize` calls become
`_standardize_causal`, where

```
z[i] = (x[i] - mean(x[0..i])) / std_pop(x[0..i])      # prefix only, no future
z[i] = 0.0  when i == 0 or std_pop(x[0..i]) == 0.0
```

Everything else is byte-identical: the same 30d trailing avg/vol (already
causal), the same fixed a-priori boundaries (`_LEVEL_HI=0.5`,
`_LEVEL_LO=-0.5`, `_VOL_HI=1.0`), the same `i < avg_lookback → NEAR_ZERO`
burn-in. **No additional burn-in and no minimum-prefix parameter is
introduced** — inventing one would be a free parameter, i.e. tuning.
`near_zero` stays excluded.

**Variant B — drop the regime filter entirely.**
`excluded_regimes = ()`. Labels for *reporting* come from the ORIGINAL
`classify_span` (the filter is off, so the labeller cannot affect trade
selection; using the original keeps B a clean one-lever delta from frozen).
This is the "was the filter buying anything real" control.

Note B is **not** a superset of A: `simulate_symbol_scaled` advances
`i = exit_idx` after a fill, so a `near_zero` entry in B can consume the 7
days that would have carried a genuine-regime entry in A. Trade sets are
compared, not nested.

| **G-DET** | scorer run twice, byte-compare the emitted JSON | identical bytes; no wall-clock in provenance; `math.fsum`; seeds pinned. |

### G3 test statistic (defined before running)

1. Partition the aligned span into **non-overlapping 90-day calendar
   windows** anchored at the span's first date. Window index for a trade =
   `(entry_date - span_first).days // 90`.
2. For window `w`, let `meanA_w` = mean net PnL per trade of A's trades with
   entry in `w`, and `meanB_w` likewise.
3. Include window `w` iff **both** A and B have ≥ 1 trade in it. Report the
   number of windows dropped and why.
4. `Δ_w = meanA_w − meanB_w`.
5. Bootstrap: resample the included windows **with replacement**, 10,000
   iterations, `random.Random(20260819)`; statistic = `mean(Δ_w)` over the
   resample; CI95 = 2.5th/97.5th percentiles.

Secondary (reported, not gated): pooled mean-per-trade Δ, total PnL Δ, n Δ.

### Supporting numbers

- **Label flips:** 560 / 2,341 dates (23.9%) change regime label under causal
  standardization; **498 / 2,341 (21.3%)** flip trade/no-trade. This
  independently reproduces V271's 21.5% finding.
- **Ledger survival:** only **60.2%** of the frozen ledger's 1,225 trades
  (738) survive causal labelling. A also takes **607 trades the frozen ledger
  never took**. The two books share barely half their rows.
- **Regime composition collapses:** frozen splits 561 negative_carry / 324
  positive_carry / 340 high_vol; causal-A splits **970 / 160 / 215**. The
  expanding-window z is dragged by an early-history prefix mean, so the
  classifier is not a noisier version of the original — it is a different
  classifier.
- **Median edge in bps of one leg's notional:** frozen **5.98 bps**,
  causal-A **1.32 bps**, no-filter-B **1.47 bps**. For context (arithmetic on
  already-measured numbers, not a new gate): V270 measured a realized
  half-spread of **0.4650 bps** [0.305, 0.590], and the trade has **4 fills**
  (2 legs × entry/exit) ⇒ ~**1.86 bps** of unmodelled slippage. Frozen's
  5.98 bps absorbs that; causal-A's 1.32 bps does not.
- **Note on the brief's total:** the frozen ledger totals **$44,311.42** here,
  not the $29,482 quoted in the session brief. n and median match to the
  digit, so the join is right; the total moved with the V255.D-EXT extension

## 8. Conclusion

**VERDICT: REFUTES V255.C's live-deployability.** This is the pre-registered
"A degrades toward B" branch of §4, and it lands on it cleanly: A's median
($0.34) sits essentially on top of B's ($0.30), their CI95s overlap almost
completely, and both are roughly a sixth of the frozen ledger's $1.95.

The pair-comparison is what makes this readable. G1 alone would only say
"causal labelling costs money." G3 says *why*: once the labeller can no longer
see the future, keeping the regime filter is statistically indistinguishable
from throwing it away (Δ CI95 [−$0.63, +$9.18], contains zero). The filter was
not a mechanism that got noisier — it was a mechanism whose entire measured
contribution ran through the full-span standardization. The composition shift
(negative_carry 561 → 970 while positive_carry 324 → 160) is the same fact
seen structurally: the "regimes" the frozen book traded are not identifiable
in real time.

What is **not** refuted: funding carry itself. A's CI95 still excludes zero on
the positive side, so a causally-computable, filter-free carry rule does have
a measurable positive median. But at 1.3–1.5 bps per trade it sits **below**
V270's measured 4-fill spread cost of ~1.86 bps, so the honest reading is that
the deployable residue of this lane is inside its own execution friction. The
retrospective V255.C/D ADOPT verdict stands as a statement about a scorer; it
does not survive translation into a forward rule.

Per the §5 anti-Goodhart commitment, **no V272-2 is queued** to search for a
filter variant that reproduces the frozen numbers. `classify_span_causal` is
shipped as the honest labeller and `classify_span` is now documented as
scorer-only. The standing baseline (crisis +$599 / trend +$2,997 / recent +$30)
is untouched — V272 wrote no `data/{version}_gate_result.json` and ran no
Victoria gate.
