## Hypothesis

A 90-day headless live-paper soak on the V240-selective universe (with the V253
MATIC→POL forward adjustment), driven by the V252 scheduler+checkpoint runner,
produces:

- **(a)** at least **one independent recent-regime window** — a fresh, zero-overlap
  90-day span, labelled by the *existing* offline classifier
  (`walk_forward_freeze.py:regime_label`) at window close (NOT by an online
  detector — that framing is rejected per SCOPE §2.7). This advances recent-N from
  4 → 5 independent.
- **(b)** daily PnL entries consistent with the pre-registered distribution below
  (the soak is the first genuinely **out-of-sample, un-Goodhartable** read of the
  frozen strategy).
- **(c)** zero crash-recovery failures and zero non-recoverable drift alerts across
  ≥90 days unattended.

### Pre-registered PnL distribution

`N(mean = +$3.63/cycle pooled, sd ≈ $499/cycle)`.

- **Mean** (+$3.63/cycle) is the task-registered pooled nominal from the standing

- **SD** (≈$499/cycle) is derived from the three per-regime sentinel ledgers'

  keys off **SD/SE**, not the mean level, so this discrepancy does not affect the
  drift test; the first **monthly reconciliation** locks the operational mean from
  the live per-cycle curve. Pre-registered constants live in
  `scripts/v253_weekly_audit.py` (`PREREG_MEAN_PER_CYCLE`, `PREREG_SD_PER_CYCLE`).

## Falsifiers

The soak is **REFUTED / halted-for-diagnosis** if any of:

1. **Crash without checkpoint recovery** — the daemon dies and restart does NOT
   reconstruct equity/positions/PnL byte-identically from the last checkpoint (V252
   Test B is the standing proof this shouldn't happen; a real failure is a
   checkpoint-integrity bug).
2. **Feed unavailability > 5 consecutive days** on any of the 6 info feeds + OHLCV.
   Detected by `v253_weekly_audit.py` (`max_consecutive_missing_days > 5` ⇒
   `feed_falsifier_tripped`).
3. **PnL drift > 5·SE from the pre-registered mean** (band ≈ $263/cycle). This is a
   **measurement-instrument** problem (feed drift / code-path bug), **not** an alpha
   problem — investigate the instrument, do NOT touch the strategy.
4. **Regime classifier cannot label the window at 90-day close** (a required series
   is missing) — the freeze-and-label step fails, so the window can't advance
   recent-N. Diagnose feed coverage before freezing.

## 90-day soak protocol

- **Weekly** — run `python3 scripts/v253_weekly_audit.py` (read-only): equity
  curve, cadence/checkpoint gaps, 5·SE drift alert. Investigate any ⚠, log the read.
- **Monthly** — reconciliation: replay the trailing 30 live days through the
  backtest arm (`scripts/v252_reconcile_smoke.py`-style A/B) and assert the
  live-vs-backtest Δ < 2·SE (the V251 ongoing check). A > 2·SE Δ is a FINDING to
  investigate (feed drift? code-path bug?), never a strategy edit.
- **Day 90** — freeze-and-label: `live_paper_freeze_quarter.sh` (to be built at the
  freeze step per SCOPE §7) freezes the trailing 90 live bars, labels via
  `walk_forward_freeze.py:regime_label`, appends to `walk_forward_manifest.json`,
  and advances the recent-N tracker. Update `V247_RULER.md` with the new per-regime
  N and the tightened 2·SE bars.

## Anti-Goodhart guard (load-bearing)

The 90-day soak is a **MEASUREMENT**, not a training run. **Do NOT touch strategy
code during the soak.** If live PnL diverges from the backtest by > 2·SE, that is a
**FINDING to investigate** (feed drift? as-of/bar-alignment bug? code-path
divergence?), *not* a strategy-side adjustment. The whole point of the phase
transition is that the frozen strategy is now being measured out-of-sample for the
first time; adjusting it mid-soak would destroy the only un-Goodhartable read the
campaign can get. Every parked flag (V241/V244/V245/V246/V248) stays **OFF**.
