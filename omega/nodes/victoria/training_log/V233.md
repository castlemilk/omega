additive crisis term able to actually move a trade on a broad correlated crash by changing

## Design — a 2×2 over SITE, with the existing terms as the probe

Two site changes, each default-inert behind a flag, applied to the existing crisis terms
(no new signal yet — isolate the site variable):

1. **Pre-demean / partial-demean application** (`crisis_term_predemean_enabled`): add the
   gated crisis term to the per-ticker composite **before** the cross-sectional demean (or
   blend: `composite += w·term` pre-demean), so the common-mode risk-off survives the
   demean instead of being subtracted away. This is the direct fix for the
   "demean guts the broad crisis tilt" mechanism named in `signal_generation.py:1337`.
2. **Weight escalation in-gate** (`crisis_term_gated_weight`): raise the gated weight from
   0.2 toward a value that can cross a conviction boundary on 2024aug (sweep {0.2, 0.4,
   0.6}). Pure magnitude test — does the term simply need to be louder once the gate fires?

**Probe cells (crisis-only, the V232 grid):** measure V227 skew (already validated as the
incumbent) under each site change. The key read is the **2024aug per-window Δ**: if it
moves off $0.00 *negative-PnL-reducing* (i.e. less loss) the site fix works; if it stays
$0.00 the demean/weight wasn't the wall and the obstacle is deeper (sizing/exit layer).

Only **after** a site change demonstrably moves 2024aug do we carry a *signal* through it.
The signal candidate, when that gate opens, is **Track B #2 — cross-sectional correlation
spike** (per-cycle basket mean |ρ| over a 5d lookback; spike = "everything sells together",
orthogonal to vol-of-vol and drawdown level; `math.fsum` reductions; `[-1,0]` additive).
