## Hypothesis (one sentence)

When realized trailing pairwise correlation across the traded book spikes above
a threshold τ, capping NEW-entry position sizing by a factor s (drop-only,
never up-size, existing positions untouched) compresses the correlated
grind-down that dominates the pooled left tail — tightening pooled p25 while
preserving or lifting means, particularly on recent where tail losses dominate
(+$30 mean, p25 deep negative).

## Why this bet (user's frame: "get profitable reliably")

V241 failed precisely on the tail conjunction (p25-Δ −$705) while injecting
$27,345 of pooled spread. The tail dimension has never been attacked directly:
the entire V148→V241 arc is entry/selection/sizing on per-name signals. This is
the first *portfolio-level risk control* measured on the full distribution.

## Design

- **Detector:** rolling mean pairwise correlation of daily close-to-close
  returns over a trailing **7-bar** window (V237 spec) across the ACTIVE
  universe (selective, blacklist {BTC,DOT,LINK} excluded from the book but BTC
  present in every snapshot), computed from the same frozen OHLCV the strategy
  already holds. Bar-time only (V216 fence); `math.fsum` for every cross-ticker
  reduction (V221 fence). Note: a strict 60-bar trailing corr is NOT computable
  at early-window entry bars (windows are 91 bars, entries begin ~bar 28) —
  this, not missing OHLCV, is the real constraint behind the V243 caveat; the
  frozen walk-forward snapshots DO contain full per-symbol OHLCV.
- **Trigger:** detector value > τ at the entry bar.
- **Actuator:** drop-only multiplicative cap on new-entry weights at the
  retained V234/V237 site — `raw_weights` normalization in `strategy.py` —
  `weight *= s` while triggered. Whole-book, name-blind by construction.
- **Flags:** `portfolio_corr_cap_enabled` (default **OFF** ⇒ byte-identical to
  standing main; unit-verified), `portfolio_corr_cap_threshold` (τ),
  `portfolio_corr_cap_scale` (s), `portfolio_corr_cap_lookback` (7).
- **Phase-0 tracer (mandatory, V241 lesson — activity measured before
  quality):** per-cycle log of {corr observed, τ, fired?, cap magnitude,
  entries affected} to `$OMEGA_AUDIT_OUTPUT_DIR`-relative JSONL.
