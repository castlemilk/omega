# V199 — Carry-only sub-strategy (planned)

**Date:** TBD
**Parent:** V197 (PipelineTracer) / V191 (funding-carry signal, range-gated)
**Status:** planned

## Hypothesis

Adding a **carry-only sub-strategy** that votes purely on
funding/basis signals — independent of `range_bound`, momentum, or
macro — will unlock trade flow in flat-vol regimes where the
current stack is silent (VIX low, TDA smooth, basket_std at floor).

This is the conclusion of
`docs/research/crypto-signal-effectiveness-review.md` (2026-05-24):
the existing stack is well-tuned for trending markets but has no
answer to directionless-but-information-asymmetric regimes.

## Planned changes

- New `carry_sub_strategy` in the ensemble that consumes:
  - `funding_rate_signal` (point-in-time, already built)
  - `funding_carry_signal` (V191 — currently range-gated, ungate it
    here)
  - **new** funding-rate-of-change signal (~30 lines, see below)
  - **new** cross-exchange basis signal (Coinbase vs Binance vs Bybit
    perp funding) (~80 lines, V193 WS infra exists)
- Lower size cap (~10-20% of normal) — lower edge per trade,
  compensated by higher frequency in flat markets.
- Use PipelineTracer (V197) to verify carry signals actually reach
  the composite.

## Gate plan

Run all three gates: `recent`, `trend`, `crisis`. Expectation:
- recent: should improve vs V176 high-water if carry fires in flat
  periods.
- trend: should be neutral (carry sized small in trending markets).
- crisis: unknown — carry may help or hurt depending on funding
  dislocations during sell-offs.
