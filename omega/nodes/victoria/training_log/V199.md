# V199 — Carry-only sub-strategy (planned)

**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Parent:** V197 (PipelineTracer) / V191 (funding-carry signal, range-gated)
**Status:** complete — **new recent high-water**

## Hypothesis

Adding a **carry-only sub-strategy** that votes purely on
funding/basis signals — independent of `range_bound`, momentum, or
macro — will unlock trade flow in flat-vol regimes where the
current stack is silent (VIX low, TDA smooth, basket_std at floor).

This is the conclusion of
`docs/research/crypto-signal-effectiveness-review.md` (2026-05-24):
the existing stack is well-tuned for trending markets but has no
answer to directionless-but-information-asymmetric regimes.

## Planned changes

- Inject the top-level `signals["carry"]` value into each per-ticker
  sig dict as `funding_carry_signal` so it participates in the
  ensemble vote (previously the carry signal sat at the top level
  and never reached the per-ticker composite — silent-override
  pattern from V191/V197).
- Add a **carry-only sub-strategy fallback**: when the main stack
  abstains entirely (no long *and* no short candidates) but the
  funding-carry signal has strong confidence (|annualised| ≥ 20%
  APY, `regime_tag ∈ {crowded_long, crowded_short}`), construct a
  small BTCUSDT trade in the carry direction at 15% of normal size.
- Suppressed in confirmed crisis (existing crisis-bias logic owns
  that regime).

**Scope note (yellow flag):** the rate-of-change and cross-exchange
basis signals from the original V199 plan were deferred — kept
minimal at one file (`strategy.py`) for cleaner attribution of any
delta. Those go in V200/V201 if V199's hypothesis confirms.

**Files touched:** `omega/nodes/victoria/strategy.py` only.

## Conclusion

**Confirmed for `recent`, refuted for `trend`, neutral for `crisis`.**

The carry-only sub-strategy works as designed in flat / mixed-regime
markets — `recent` PnL roughly doubles and trade count more than
doubles, exactly the "fires when others abstain" pattern the
hypothesis predicted. The new path beats V176's high-water mark.

The unexpected result is the `trend` regression: V172 ran 64 trades
for +$18,437 (PnL/trade ≈ $288); V199 runs 106 trades for +$7,864
(PnL/trade ≈ $74). The extra ~40 trades are mostly carry-injected
candidates that dilute the strong trend-stack edge. The fallback
guard (only fires when main stack abstains entirely) clearly isn't
strict enough in trending regimes — momentum is firing AND carry
is voting in the ensemble path, shifting some composites past the
threshold that shouldn't have qualified.

`crisis` is unchanged: carry doesn't fire (suppressed in crisis as
designed), and the underlying crisis trend-bias remains the open
problem.
