## Why V209 exists

V207a fenced two known leak channels (PYTHONHASHSEED=42 + frozen
funding cache) and got the crisis spread from $1,978 to $113. But it
unmasked a *third* channel: recent jumped from $0.49 to $3,257 and
trend from $0.49 to $14,047. V207b localized that channel to the
sizing-multiplier chain in `_construct_portfolio` (rolling z-score
over signal-name-keyed `_signal_history` is iteration-order
dependent). V208a applied a canonical `sorted(... key=ticker)` to
every `items()` traversal in `_construct_portfolio` and the recent
pair collapsed to $0.06 with identical trade count.

V208a only re-measured the *recent* gate. The same fix should — by
the V207b mechanism — also collapse the trend and crisis pairs,
because the channel is structural to `_construct_portfolio` and not
gate-specific. V209 is the verification: full 3-gate × 2-pair audit
at HEAD (V208a strategy.py).

**V209 is methodology-only. No strategy.py change.** Until this
audit passes, no new strategy hypothesis runs.

## Hypothesis

Full V206b 3-gate × 2-pair audit at HEAD (V208a) produces:

- within-pair PnL spread **< $100** on every gate, AND
- **identical trade count** between r1 and r2 on every gate.

If true: V208a's canonical-sort fix is sufficient to eliminate the
third channel on every gate, the three fences (PYTHONHASHSEED pin
+ frozen funding cache + canonical sort) are jointly the new noise
floor, and the V204/V205/V206b headline numbers can be re-baselined
on this floor as the new high-waters for the README.

## Procedure

For each gate ∈ {recent, trend, crisis}:

1. Record pre-run cache fingerprints (`sha256sum` on
   `data/macro_cache.db data/omega_victoria_state.db data/omega_victoria_memory.db`).
2. Run #1 with `--version v209_<gate>_r1 --cycles 200 --sleep 10 --seed 42 --backtest-snapshot data/snapshots/<snap>.json --frozen-cache`,
   stdout → `data/v209_audit/<gate>_r1/stdout.log`,
   stderr → `data/v209_audit/<gate>_r1/stderr.log`. Each run launched
   with `PYTHONHASHSEED=42` in env (V207a fence).
3. Copy `data/v209_<gate>_r1_results.json`, `_trades.csv`,
   `/tmp/v209_<gate>_r1_metrics.jsonl` into `data/v209_audit/<gate>_r1/`.
4. Run #2 with `_r2` suffix, identical other args. Save to
   `data/v209_audit/<gate>_r2/`.
5. Verify post-run fingerprints == pre-run (frozen-cache check).

All six runs sequential — never overlapping. V204 resilient launch
pattern: `nohup … & disown`. ETA ~3-4h total (per V206b pace,
~30 min/run).

Snapshot mapping (per V206b):
- recent → `data/snapshots/snap_20260414.json`
- trend → `data/snapshots/snap_trending_2023q4.json`
- crisis → `data/snapshots/snap_crisis_2022h1.json`

## Pass criteria (operational)

For each gate's r1 vs r2:

- `metrics.jsonl` (timestamp-stripped, see `tr -d` recipe):
  `md5sum` identical OR within-pair PnL spread < $100.
- `results.json`: `|PnL_r1 - PnL_r2| < $100` AND
  `closed_trades_r1 == closed_trades_r2`.
- `trades.csv` row-by-row ex-timestamp: identical OR
  `(cycle, symbol, side)` keys match on every row.
- `state.db` / `memory.db` / `decision_traces` / `attribution.json`:
  byte-identical (per V206b these were always identical; sanity
  check).
