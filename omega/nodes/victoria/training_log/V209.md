## Why V209 exists

V207a fenced two known leak channels (PYTHONHASHSEED=42 + frozen
funding cache) and got the crisis spread from $1,978 to $113. But it
unmasked a *third* channel: recent jumped from $0.49 to $3,257 and
trend from $0.49 to $14,047. V207b localized that channel to the
sizing-multiplier chain in `_construct_portfolio` (rolling z-score
over signal-name-keyed `_signal_history` is iteration-order
dependent). V208a applied a canonical `sorted(... key=ticker)` to
every `items()` traversal in `_construct_portfolio` and the recent
pair collapsed to $0.06 with identical trade count.

V208a only re-measured the *recent* gate. The same fix should — by
the V207b mechanism — also collapse the trend and crisis pairs,
because the channel is structural to `_construct_portfolio` and not
gate-specific. V209 is the verification: full 3-gate × 2-pair audit
at HEAD (V208a strategy.py).

**V209 is methodology-only. No strategy.py change.** Until this
audit passes, no new strategy hypothesis runs.

## Hypothesis

Full V206b 3-gate × 2-pair audit at HEAD (V208a) produces:

- within-pair PnL spread **< $100** on every gate, AND
- **identical trade count** between r1 and r2 on every gate.

If true: V208a's canonical-sort fix is sufficient to eliminate the
third channel on every gate, the three fences (PYTHONHASHSEED pin
+ frozen funding cache + canonical sort) are jointly the new noise
floor, and the V204/V205/V206b headline numbers can be re-baselined
on this floor as the new high-waters for the README.

## Conclusion

**Partial pass. Falsifier branch 2 fires.**

Crisis passed: $56 within-pair spread with identical trade count is
a 35× improvement over V206b's $1,978 crisis floor and lands well
inside the $100 target. The three fences (PYTHONHASHSEED + frozen
funding cache + canonical sort) are jointly sufficient for the
crisis path.

Recent and trend failed: spreads of $2,773 and $1,500 with trade Δ
of 3 each. The V208a recent finding ($0.06 with Δ=0) did **not**
reproduce — V209 recent_r1 reproduces V208a r1 to within $0.03
(−$2,292.98 vs V208a's −$2,292.95), but V209 r2 = +$480.51 vs
V208a's r2 = −$2,292.89. **The V208a single-pair result was either
a transient fluke or its environment differed in a way the audit
script did not capture.** A second, gate-specific non-determinism
channel is alive on recent and trend.

The cycle-1 composite divergence on recent (1e-4) is the same
magnitude V206b reported on the same gate before any fix. Either:
1. The canonical sort in `_construct_portfolio` doesn't actually
   affect the signal composite formation (which happens upstream in
   `_compute_composite_score` / sub-signal layer), and we were
   measuring a downstream sizing-chain channel that V208a
   eliminated. The signal-composite-formation channel still bleeds.
2. There's another iteration-order-sensitive dict traversal upstream
   of `_construct_portfolio` that V208a didn't touch.

Both hypotheses are testable in V210 with cycle-1 sub-signal trace
bisect: any signal whose **per-symbol per-cycle value** differs
between r1 and r2 at cycle 1 is the source. The bisect target is
`signal_contribs.jsonl` row-by-row at cycle 1 across r1 and r2.
