## 1. Why this version exists

33 V###s of alpha search have produced ~16 refutations and exactly **two validated
lanes**. They have only ever been measured **independently**:

| Lane | Standing result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **A — spot Victoria** (V240 selective universe, blacklist {BTC, DOT, LINK}) | crisis +$599 / trend +$2,997 / recent +$30 (nominal-N) | STANDING BASELINE |
| **B — funding-carry V255.C** (V255.D-EXT frozen real basis, 90% real-priced) | pooled median +$1.95/trade, CI95 [+$1.13, +$2.80] | ADOPT, KEEP-FLAG-GATED |

**The never-asked question:** what happens at the **portfolio** level? Three
mutually-exclusive possibilities, and the campaign has evidence for none of them:

- **Diversify** — uncorrelated PnL streams, risk-adjusted lift.
- **Interfere** — both long-biased crypto, doubled beta, risk-adjusted *worse*.
- **Compose optimally** — some weighted mix beats either alone.

This is a **$0 analysis on committed ledgers**. No new grids, no compute burn.

## 2. Hypotheses

- **H1** — ρ(Victoria daily PnL, V255.C daily PnL) < 0.5 (substantially uncorrelated).
- **H2** — optimally-weighted combined Sharpe > 1.1× best single-lane Sharpe.
- **H3** — worst-window drawdown of combined < worst-window drawdown of either alone.

**Outcome mapping:**

- **ADOPT** — all 3 pass. Portfolio combination validated; queue a V266-2

- **CAVEATED** — some pass. Honest note on *which* composition benefit is real.
- **STOP** — none pass. The lanes are the same trade in different clothes; the alpha
  search has been double-counting and the campaign is done at portfolio level.

## 4. Method (fixed in advance)

1. **Align to a common daily PnL series.**
   - Victoria: per-window `*_r1_trades.csv` from the V240 `universe_selective`
     confirm grid. Map each trade's exit `cycle` to a calendar date via the replay
     geometry (`providers/replay.py`: cursor starts at `window`=28, so the decision
     bar for cycle *c* is series index `26 + c`). **The wall-clock `timestamp`
     column is the run time, not the simulated bar — it must not be used.**
   - Use only the **26 independent primary windows**. The 6 `_recent_supplements`
     overlap their primary neighbours by 45d and would double-count days (V249
     independent-N rule).
   - Funding: aggregate `pnl_usd` by `exit_date` from the V255.D-EXT **frozen**
     real-basis ledger.
   - Days inside a walk-forward window with no trade are genuine **$0**; days
     outside every window are **unobserved** and are excluded — zero-filling them
     would manufacture correlation.
2. **G1** — Pearson ρ + 10k paired bootstrap CI95; Spearman ρ for robustness.
3. **G2** — annualised Sharpe (×√365) per lane; 50/50 equal-weight; risk-parity
   (∝ 1/σ); mean-variance tangency.
4. **G3** — max peak-to-trough drawdown of the cumulative PnL curve, per lane and
   combined. Because the lanes run at very different notional, also report a
   **σ-normalised** diagnostic (each lane rescaled to unit daily σ before combining).
5. **Verdict.**

## 6. What would convince us the bet was wrong

If ρ > 0.5 the two lanes are one trade wearing two hats, and every "independent
lane" claim since V254 has been double-counting the same exposure. That is a
**campaign-level** finding, not a mechanism refutation, and it would mean the
ceiling of validated alpha is materially lower than the two-lane story implies.
