## Hypothesis

The 2,805-trade gap-analysis in `docs/research/live-gap-analysis.md`
identified specific failure modes: certain symbols always lose,
holds are too short, post-crisis re-entries are too eager. Adding
symbol blacklist + min hold + damp-hours after losses should
selectively suppress those.

## Changes

- `symbol_blacklist` — drop symbols with consistent net-negative IC
- `min_hold` — block exits before N cycles
- `damp_hours` — reduce sizing for N hours after a losing trade
- V186 LLM tie-breaker carried in (later proven dead weight)
- V187 dynamic graph signal carried in (threshold never trips)
- V188 pyramid preset carried in

## Conclusion

❌ **Gate stacking has hit diminishing returns.** PF 1.04 means the
entry/exit gates are doing so much work that nothing fires — and
when something does, it's barely profitable.

Stacked gates active at this point:
- V137 FFG (first-flow gate)
- V178 normal_regime_dampen
- V181 ensemble_block_normal_shorts
- V183 normal-high-conv block
- V189 symbol_blacklist + min_hold + damp_hours

Lesson: **don't add more entry gates.** The next architectural move
needs to *unblock* trades in flat-vol regimes, not block more.
