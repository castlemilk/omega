## Conclusion

**REFUTED.** mean-Δ = −$345.82 ≤ 0 (and min-Δ = −$1,037.46) — the falsifier bar
(mean-Δ>0 AND min-Δ>0) is failed on both clauses. The RV-term-structure brake is *worse*
than the V227 skew it sits on top of: it is **inert where it should help** (2020q1,
2024aug) and **actively harmful where it fires** (2022h1, −$1,037 at an unchanged 33-trade
count). This is a stronger refutation than V227's: V227's skew at least *helped* 2022h1
(+$630); the brake *hurts* it.

**Why (the Track A pre-build trace, now confirmed by the grid):**
1. **2024aug Δ == $0.00 (GATE INERT, confirmed).** The brake reuses the V227
   drawdown-AND-gate, which on the slow yen-carry window is *decision-inert* — the gate
   fires but the post-demean, 0.2-weighted nudge never crosses a trade-decision boundary.
   Both V227 skew AND the V232 brake produce exactly $0.00 there. **2024aug is
   structurally immune to ANY post-demean weight-0.2 additive crisis term** — the obstacle
   is the application *site/weight*, not the signal's orthogonality.
2. **2020q1 Δ == $0.00.** Brake inert (the +$15.9k 7/13-universe outlier window; the gate
   evidently didn't shift a trade here either).
3. **2022h1 Δ == −$1,037.** The one window where the brake *fired* and moved PnL, it
   moved it the **wrong way** — the RV-inversion term braked positions that would have
   recovered, net-costing $1,037 at the same trade count. RV term-structure inversion is
   a *generic large-move* detector (it fires on rallies too — Track A: 60% in trend vs
   34% in crisis); even inside the crisis gate it does not reliably mark *downside* onset.

The signal hypothesis (vol-of-vol leads drawdown) is not supported by the eval: where the
brake could act, it mis-timed; where it couldn't act (the application-site wall), it was
inert. **Do not ship.** Keep `rv_term_brake_enabled` default OFF.
