## Hypothesis (one sentence)

The effective 4-name universe (ETH/ADA/NEAR/ARB) was a silent consequence of
`_TRADING_BLACKLIST`, never a measured decision; flipping to the full 13-name
snapshot universe on the V238 baseline (`universe_full` vs `universe_legacy`
cells, universe as the only variable) measures whether breadth adds
cross-sectional spread — the thing a demeaned momentum book fundamentally
harvests.

## Baseline (which V238 config)

V238 did **NOT** adopt series-ON (kept flag-gated), so the V239 baseline is the
**V238 `main` = V235 standing main = `frozen_series` OFF**. Both V239 arms run
frozen_series OFF; the only variable is the universe. The `universe_legacy` arm
must therefore be byte-identical to the V238 `main` cells (aggregator asserts

## Mechanism

New feature flag `universe_full_enabled` (default OFF, `features.py`). When ON,
the two `_construct_portfolio` blacklist consult sites (`strategy.py:2335` +
`:3652`) treat `_TRADING_BLACKLIST` as empty, opening the universe to the 9
blacklisted names (BTC/DOT/MATIC/XRP/SOL/AVAX/LINK/BNB/SUI) per
`V235_UNIVERSE_REVIEW.md` (recommended new blacklist = ∅; all 9 exclusions
noise-founded on ~99 pre-hermetic trades). Names with missing bars in a given
window are skipped automatically (MATIC→POL thin-bar caveat handled as data
coverage, not a strategy exclusion). Default OFF ⇒ byte-identical 4-name
baseline (unit-verified + smoke-verified below). `_LONG_BLACKLIST={BTCUSDT}`
is NOT flipped (kept out of scope to hold "universe as the only variable" — BTC
can short but not long under the flip).
