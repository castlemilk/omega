- **Crisis spread tightened to $6–12** (vs V210's $28 σ), Δtrades = 0 across all 4 runs.

**Crisis headline shifted, not regressed.** V211's full canonicalization moves crisis from V209's deterministic-but-partially-canonicalized −$17,763 (65 trades) to −$24,834 (62 trades). Same WR (0.323), same PF (0.512), 3 fewer trades. V210 predicted this asymmetrically: cycle-1 composite drift in crisis was 1e-2 (100× larger than recent's 1e-4); under partial canonicalization that drift happened to leave selection stable within a single V209 pair, but the basket-aggregation sort changes *which* deterministic answer the system computes. V208a's −$17,763 was one canonical point under partial fencing; V211's −$24,834 is the canonical point under full basket-level fencing. Both are deterministic; the latter is the structurally correct one to anchor going forward.

**Residual non-determinism.** Trend p1_r2 dropped one trade vs the other 3 trend runs (105 vs 106). All else identical. This is a fourth, much smaller channel — not blocking pre-registered acceptance, but worth chasing.
