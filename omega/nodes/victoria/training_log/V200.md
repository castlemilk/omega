**Date:** 2026-05-28

**Status:** planned

## Hypothesis

V199 broke the `recent` high-water (+$2,478) but regressed `trend`
(+$7,864 vs V172 +$18,437) — the per-ticker `funding_carry_signal`
injection diluted the trend-stack edge by pushing additional
composites past threshold in bull/bear regimes.

V200 adds a **trend-regime suppressor** on the carry vote: when the
HMM reports `bull` or `bear` with confidence > 0.5, skip injecting
`funding_carry_signal` into the per-ticker ensemble. The carry-only
sub-strategy fallback (which only fires when the main stack abstains
entirely) is left untouched — it can still pick up genuine
information-asymmetric setups in trending markets when the main stack
has nothing to say.

Goal: recover V172's trend PnL without sacrificing V199's recent gain.
