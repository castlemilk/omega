**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Author:** claude

**Status:** complete — **hypothesis refuted, recent regressed**

## Hypothesis

V199 broke the `recent` high-water (+$2,478) but regressed `trend`
(+$7,864 vs V172 +$18,437) — the per-ticker `funding_carry_signal`
injection diluted the trend-stack edge by pushing additional
composites past threshold in bull/bear regimes.

V200 adds a **trend-regime suppressor** on the carry vote: when the
HMM reports `bull` or `bear` with confidence > 0.5, skip injecting
`funding_carry_signal` into the per-ticker ensemble. The carry-only
sub-strategy fallback (which only fires when the main stack abstains
entirely) is left untouched — it can still pick up genuine
information-asymmetric setups in trending markets when the main stack
has nothing to say.

Goal: recover V172's trend PnL without sacrificing V199's recent gain.

## Conclusion

**Hypothesis refuted; recent regressed; no high-water broken.**

Two findings, one diagnostic flag:

1. **Trend gate identical to V199** (106 trades both runs, PnL within
   0.6%). The trend-regime suppressor essentially never fired on the
   trend snapshot — strong evidence that HMM in the backtest replay
   doesn't hit `bull|bear` with confidence > 0.5 on
   `snap_trending_2023q4.json`. The trend snapshot must be classified
   as `sideways` or low-confidence by the HMM despite being a
   trending market. So the V199 trend regression cannot be the
   per-ticker carry injection driven by HMM-confirmed trend — it's
   coming from somewhere else.

2. **Recent gate collapsed** (+$2,478 → +$427) despite the suppressor
   being designed to be a no-op in the recent regime. Trade count
   moved +3 (67 → 70), so the carry path is still firing — but
   per-trade PnL crashed from $37 to $6. Either (a) HMM did flag
   bull/bear with conf>0.5 on parts of the recent snapshot, and
   suppressing carry there dropped the high-PnL trades, or (b) it's
   variance from a different code path (no deterministic seed across
   the carry/regime read order). The +3 trade delta argues against
   pure variance.

3. **The V199 trend regression diagnosis was wrong.** V199's "next
   steps" assumed momentum + carry were both firing in the
   per-ticker ensemble during trending markets. With the suppressor
   not firing on the trend snapshot, the extra trades vs V172 must
   come from one of: (i) the carry-only fallback (only fires when
   main stack abstains — possible the main stack abstains more often
   on this snapshot than expected), (ii) the per-ticker injection
   firing under HMM=`sideways`/unknown labels, or (iii) an unrelated
   change between V172 and V199 in the ensemble voter / threshold
   stack.

**High-water table:** no records broken. README.md not updated.
