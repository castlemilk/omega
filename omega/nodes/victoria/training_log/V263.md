## 1. Why this version exists

Every V### since V241 has been a variation on the **same object**: the existing
Victoria signal composite. Different reviewers (V241 whole-basket LLM, V258
specialist ensemble), different sizings (V243), added signals (V244, V245, V259,
V260, V261), different exits (V246), different frequencies (V262, V262-2). The
V241–V258 retrospective's own taxonomy says the meta-cause is R3 (calendar-bound
N) with R2 (below-resolution effects) dominating — but every one of those bets
still drew its information from the same feature family.

Kronos is the first candidate in the campaign that is **not** a re-weighting of
that family. It is a decoder-only transformer pretrained on K-line (OHLCV) data
from 45+ global exchanges, which tokenizes OHLCV into hierarchical discrete
tokens and autoregressively forecasts future bars. Its prior comes from
cross-market price-pattern statistics the Victoria composite has never seen.

This is the closest thing the campaign has to a genuinely orthogonal signal
class. It is also, on base rates, a long shot: foundation models on financial
data have a decent track record for **pattern capture** and a poor one for
**tradeable alpha after friction**.

## 2. Alpha thesis (pre-declared)

> A transformer pretrained on 45+ exchanges' K-line data has internalised
> cross-market price-pattern statistics that generalise to our 13-name crypto
> universe. If so, its forecast of the next H hours should beat a naive
> random-walk baseline on error, and its forecast **direction** should correlate
> with realized direction better than chance — giving a directional bias that can
> feed the composite as a z-score input or stand alone as an entry signal.

### 2a. The load-bearing assumption

**Forecast accuracy is not alpha.** A model can beat a naive baseline on RMSE and
still be untradeable, because RMSE is dominated by the *level* path while PnL is
determined by the *sign* of the move net of friction. The V262-2 refutation is the
cautionary precedent: a real ~14 bps 1h mean-reversion effect that sat below the
24 bps friction wall at every hold.

Conversely — and this is what the Phase-1 smoke tests — a model that fails on
*both* error and direction has nothing to trade, and no amount of downstream
scorer engineering rescues it. That makes the direction/error check the correct
cheap Phase-0 separator (standing rule from V234: no grid until a probe shows the
variable discriminates).

### 3a. Signal derivation for the eventual strategy

Two candidate wirings, both declared before the smoke:

1. **Composite input** — forecast H-bar return → cross-sectional z-score →
   an additional member of the existing composite, weight-gated like any other
   signal.
2. **Standalone entry signal** — forecast direction + magnitude as its own
   entry rule, scored as an independent strategy (the Track-B pattern that
   worked for funding-carry).

(2) is preferred if it clears, because it manufactures **independent N** rather
than adding signal to a composite judged at the N=4 noise floor — the exact
failure mode the retrospective assigns to Tracks E and F.

## 4. Falsifiers — LOCKED, no post-hoc tuning

Declared in the task brief **before any code ran**. F1–F3 apply to the eventual
V263-2 scorer; **F4 is evaluatable at smoke stage and is the gate on everything
downstream.**

| ID | Falsifier | Bar | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | Pooled median PnL > $0, CI95 excludes zero | median > 0 AND CI95 excl. 0 | V263-2 |
| **F2** | MWU p < 0.05 with N_eff deflation (SLEM 0.78 from V262 F4b) | p_deflated < 0.05 | V263-2 |
| **F3** | Annualized net ≥ 15% at the 1h fee model (24 bps round-trip) | ≥ 15% | V263-2 |
| **F4** | Forecast direction correlates with realized direction better than random | **pooled Spearman ρ > 0.05** | **V263 smoke** |

### Anti-Goodhart controls

- Universe, threshold, hold and fee are locked **pre-scorer** (§3).
- The horizon sweep is pre-declared as `{1, 4, 12, 24}`; picking the best cell
  post-hoc is a multiple-comparisons violation, so every cell is reported and a
  **Bonferroni correction across all cells** is applied. No cherry-picking.
- Directional accuracy is reported **alongside its drift confound** (realized
  up-rate vs forecast up-rate) plus balanced accuracy and MCC, because raw
  directional accuracy on a drifting asset is not a skill measure.
- Sampling is seeded; identical arguments reproduce forecasts bit-for-bit.

## 5. Phase-1 smoke result (summary — full detail in the verdict doc)

**F4 FAILED.** Mean Spearman across 8 pre-declared cells = **−0.027** vs the
+0.05 bar. 0 of 8 cells beat the naive baseline on RMSE. No cell survives
Bonferroni correction.

Because F4 is the pre-declared gate on the whole thesis, **V263-2 is NOT
pre-registered and NOT queued.** F1–F3 are recorded above for the record only;
they were never run, and building a scorer against a model with no measured
directional edge would be exactly the "mechanism against a below-resolution
objective" pattern the retrospective identifies as the campaign's dominant waste.
