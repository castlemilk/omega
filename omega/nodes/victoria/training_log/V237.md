## Hypothesis (one sentence)

Crisis losses are portfolio-level (correlated basket grind-down with no
cross-sectional spread to harvest — V231/V234 forensics), so a **whole-book
throttle triggered by a cross-ticker correlation spike** will cut the crisis
distribution's left tail where every per-name gate (signal/site/weight/sizing/
selection) could not.

## Design

- **Detector:** rolling mean pairwise correlation of daily returns across the
  active universe over a trailing 7-bar window, computed from the same frozen
  OHLCV the strategy already holds (Track B #2 confirmed computability in all
  windows). Bar-time only (V216 fence discipline); `math.fsum` for every
  cross-ticker reduction (V221 discipline).
- **Trigger:** corr > threshold C (grid: C ∈ {0.75, 0.85}) ⇒ throttle **NEW**
  position entries to 0 for the next K cycles (grid: K ∈ {5, 10}); existing
  positions unaffected (this is an exposure cap, not a liquidation rule).
- **Actuator:** the retained V234 site — `raw_weights` normalization in
  `strategy.py` (~3384) — scaling/zeroing the WHOLE book's new-entry weights, so
  the mechanism cannot be blind to which name loses (the exact blindness that
  killed V227–V234).
- **Flags:** `portfolio_corr_cap_enabled` (default OFF ⇒ byte-identical standing
  main), `portfolio_corr_cap_threshold`, `portfolio_corr_cap_cycles`.
  `assert_cell_identity` gains `--expect-corrcap` + a fired-cycles observability
  counter (fired==0 on a window is legitimate; the flag value is the identity).

## Pre-work (mandatory, before any grid — the V234 standing rule)

Env-gated probe on the walk-forward crisis windows: log per-cycle mean pairwise
correlation and verify the detector actually discriminates — i.e. corr spikes
co-occur with the losing entry clusters on ≥2 of the crisis-labeled windows
(binding: the windows where `main` loses). **No separator ⇒ no grid** — log the
refutation at probe cost (~2 short runs).

## Falsifier (pre-committed, on the V235 walk-forward distribution)

Measured against the V235 `main` crisis distribution (N=7 windows — not the old
3-window set, and never a single window):

1. **WIN:** mean-Δ_crisis > +$500 AND Δtrades ≠ 0 on ≥2 crisis windows AND
   neither mean-Δ_trend nor mean-Δ_recent regresses by more than $200
   ⇒ SHIP crisis-gated.
2. **Tail-only win:** mean-Δ_crisis ∈ [0, +$500] but min (worst crisis window)
   improves > +$1,000 without trend/recent regression > $200 ⇒ ship as a
   risk-control (the objective is the left tail, per Lens 3i's 20%).
3. **FAIL (any of):** no separator in pre-work; mean-Δ_crisis < 0; trend/recent
   regression > $200 ⇒ **crisis program CLOSES** (9th refutation). Crisis becomes
   a monitored report in every future walk-forward run; no further crisis
   optimization targets may be pre-registered without new data feeds (options/
   funding history) or a universe change (V238).
4. **Determinism FAIL** ⇒ bisect/fence/re-run before any verdict (standard arc).
