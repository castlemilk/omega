## 1. The one question

V269 landed a real per-minute quoted-spread artefact and deliberately declined to
score it, reporting only a QC observation (pooled median half-spread **0.480 bps**
vs V267 G2's budget **1.6475 bps**) computed over *all retained minutes* — not at
the ledger's trades.

**V270 answers, at V255.C's actual traded symbol-days:** does the realized
half-spread **confirm**, **tighten**, or **refute** V267 G2's
`slippage_to_median_zero_bps = 1.6475`?

This is a confirmation pass on an existing number. It cannot move the standing
baseline (crisis +$599 / trend +$2,997 / recent +$30) and does not attempt to.

## 2. What V267's 1.6475 actually is (fixing the unit before comparing)

From `scripts/v267_capacity.py`:

```
edge_bps           = 1e4 * pnl / notional          # line 228
SLIPPAGE_LEG_MULT  = 4.0                           # line 55: 2 legs x (entry + exit)
slippage_to_median_zero_bps  solves  median(edge_bps) - 4s = 0
```

So **`s = 1.6475 bps` is a PER-CROSSING cost** (one leg, one side), and
`4 x 1.6475 = 6.59 bps` = V267's pooled median edge. The correctly-typed
comparison is therefore **realized half-spread per crossing vs 1.6475 bps** — a
half-spread *is* the cost of crossing the touch once. Leg-count cancels out of
G1/G2 entirely; it only affects the derived net-PnL colour in §7.

## 3. Declared deviations from the brief's scaffold (before running)

Both are forced by the artefacts, and both are stated here rather than discovered
in the results.

### 3.1 The join is SYMBOL-DAY, not timestamp — the ledger has no intraday time

`data/v269_ledger/v255c_trades.csv` carries `entry_date` / `exit_date` as bare
dates (`2020-01-31`), 1,225 rows, zero time component. V255.C is a **daily-bar**
strategy; a minute-exact join is not merely unavailable, it is **undefined** —
the strategy never chose a minute.

Rather than invent one, V270 joins at symbol-day and treats intraday timing as
the uncertainty it is:

- **point estimate** = that symbol-day's **median** of the per-minute `sp_bps_p50`
- **pessimistic variant** = that symbol-day's **p75** and **p90**, reported
  alongside, because a daily strategy does not get to pick the cheapest minute.

This is a resolution downgrade, not a fabrication. It is **not** scored as R4:
the artefact joins cleanly at the resolution the ledger actually has.

### 3.2 G4 as briefed is not a valid check — restated

The brief asks the joinable subset to "reproduce V267 G2's per-trade net PnL
median to within 1%". It cannot and should not: the joinable subset is a
**different, smaller, later-era subset** (117 of 1,225 trades, all 2023-05→2024-04)
and has no obligation to match a pooled 2020→2026 median. Requiring it to would
be a broken test that fails on a correct join.

**G4 is restated to do the job it was meant to do — validate the re-implementation:**

- **G4a (rule fidelity):** re-implement V267's rule and reproduce, on the **full
  1,225-trade ledger**, `median_edge_bps = 6.587` and
  `slippage_to_median_zero_bps = 1.6475`, each within **1%**. If this fails, the
  scorer is wrong and G1/G2 are meaningless.
- **G4b (representativeness, diagnostic — no bar):** report the joinable subset's
  own median edge vs pooled, so the reader can see how atypical the covered
  window is. A large gap does not fail V270; it bounds what G1/G2 generalise to.

## 4. Pre-registered gates

| Gate | Statistic | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** | pooled median realized half-spread per crossing at traded symbol-days, cluster-bootstrap CI95 | **≤ 1.6475 bps** |
| **G2** | per-symbol median half-spread | **no symbol's median > 1.6475 bps** |
| **G3** | coverage honesty | reported in full, loudly; not pass/fail |
| **G4a** | rule fidelity vs V267 published numbers | within **1%** |

**G1 method.** Each joinable trade contributes **two crossings** (entry-day and
exit-day). Pooled median is taken over all crossings. CI95 by **cluster bootstrap
resampling at the TRADE level** (10,000 resamples, seed 42) — entry and exit of
the same trade are dependent and must not be resampled independently.

**Trade is joinable iff BOTH its entry-day and its exit-day are present in the
artefact for its symbol.** Entry-only trades are counted and excluded, never
half-filled.

## 5. Outcome mapping (fixed before results are seen)

| Condition | Verdict |
|---|---|
| G1 median ≤ 1.6475 with CI95 upper bound also ≤ 1.6475 | **CONFIRMS V267 G2** |
| G1 median materially < 1.6475 (CI95 upper < half the budget) | **CONFIRMS, and TIGHTENS** — the real breakeven is looser than V267 assumed |
| G1 median > 1.6475, CI95 excludes the bar | **REFUTES V267 G2** |
| G1 CI95 spans 1.6475 | **R2 (below-resolution)** — not adjudicable at this N |
| artefact will not join / coverage collapses | **R4** |

## 6. Anti-Goodhart declarations

- **Coverage is already known to be worse than V269 reported, and this is
  declared here, before scoring.** V269 §5 cites 12.6% (154/1,225) joinable. That
  figure is a *calendar-month* rule. The **strict both-days join is 117/1,225 =
  9.55%** — V269's headline overstates the usable set by 24%. V270 scores the 117
  and reports the correction as a finding.
  This is a *smaller N*, not an unfit artefact, so it is **not** a STOP: the
  measurement remains well-defined. Had the join collapsed structurally
  (schema mismatch, no overlap), V270 would have stopped at R4.
- **No re-freeze, no backfill, no new download in this session.** The 259 absent
  symbol-days and the 233 permanently-unavailable April days stay absent. Any
  acquisition would be V271.
- **The artefact measures the PERP leg only.** V255.C is basis-hedged (perp +
  spot); Binance USD-M `bookTicker` says nothing about the spot leg. §7 reports a
  measured-perp lower bound and, separately labelled as an **ASSUMPTION not a
  measurement**, a symmetric spot==perp variant. G1/G2 are per-crossing and so are
  unaffected either way.
- **No bar may move after results are seen.** Verdict is computed mechanically
  from the gate booleans by the scorer.

## 7. Derived colour (reported, NOT gated)

Net per-trade edge on the joinable subset after charging the **measured** spread:

- **C1 lower bound** — charge measured perp half-spread on entry + exit
  (2 crossings), spot legs at 0.
- **C2 symmetric ASSUMPTION** — spot half-spread == perp half-spread (4 crossings).

Both reported against the subset's gross median edge. Neither is a gate; neither
is an impact model. Depth-1 cannot produce one.
