### 3a. Composite members and their signs — declared here, with provenance

The composite is the **mean of signed z-scores** of its members, on the z-score
scale (the V261 construction, `on_chain_flow/signals.py:composite_z_series`).

| Member | Native cadence | Sign | Provenance of the sign |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `hourly_return_z` | 1h (native) | **±1 — see §3b** | The one genuine fork; resolved by two pre-declared arms, not by choosing |
| `hourly_volume_z` | 1h (native) | **n/a** | Non-directional; enters as a pre-declared **entry filter**, not a signed member (§4) |
| `funding` | daily (frozen series is daily) | **−1** | Inherited from V255.C's *confirmed* funding-carry direction: positive funding ⇒ crowded longs ⇒ short |
| `open_interest` | daily | **−1** | Rising OI into a move = crowding (the V240.B whale-flow crowding read) |
| `fear_greed` | daily | **−1** | Contrarian sentiment; matches Victoria's own documented note that fear_greed is "coded bullish in bear market" and needs damping (`features.py:1827`) |
| `vix` | daily | **−1** | Risk-off ⇒ crypto bearish |
| `dxy` | daily | **−1** | Strong dollar ⇒ crypto bearish |
| `yield_curve` (DGS10 − DGS2) | daily | **+1** | Steepening ⇒ risk-on |
| `gdelt` (mean of the 5 `gdelt_tone_*` series) | daily | **+1** | Positive news tone ⇒ risk-on |

**Honesty note on these signs:** the funding sign is *inherited* from a confirmed
result (V255.C). The other six are **declared here on economic grounds**, not
transplanted from Victoria's live signal classes — those classes embed
regime-conditional weighting that cannot be lifted into an offline 1h scorer without
importing strategy code, which this task's guardrails forbid. They are fixed before
the run, which is what pre-registration requires; they are not claimed to be
Victoria's exact live conventions.

**Rebasing rule (V262.md §5, verbatim):** forward-fill at the source cadence, "no
interpolation, no look-ahead — the value in force at bar open is the last value
published strictly before it." A daily value dated *D* is in force for bars from
`D+1 00:00Z` onward (never during day *D* itself — that would be look-ahead).

**Coverage fence (all-or-nothing, the V261 rule):** a bar carries a composite only
when **every** member has a valid value at that bar. In particular, forward-fill
never extends past a feed's own `last_date`, so bars after the earliest feed
exhaustion carry no composite rather than a stale one. Degenerate z-windows
(`std == 0`) contribute no signal — a **semantic** fence, never an epsilon (the V221
epsilon-guard-amplifier lesson).

### 3b. The direction fork, and the multiplicity correction for it

At 1h resolution the sign of `hourly_return_z` is **not determined a priori**:
momentum (trend continuation) and mean-reversion (microstructure bounce) are both
standard priors. Picking one after seeing results would be exactly the Goodhart
vector V262.md §4 warns about. So **both are pre-declared as arms**:

| Arm | `hourly_return_z` sign | Reading |
|---|---:|---|
| **M** (momentum) | **+1** | 1h returns continue |
| **R** (reversion) | **−1** | 1h returns revert |

**Multiplicity correction, declared now:** testing 2 arms doubles the false-positive
rate, so **F2's significance threshold is Bonferroni-corrected to α = 0.05 / 2 =
0.025** (on top of the N_eff deflation in §6). A verdict of ADOPT requires
**F1 + F2 + F3 all to pass within the SAME arm** — passes split across arms do not
combine.

**Native-cadence z clarification (declared before the run, no result seen).** A
member's 30-observation z-window is measured in that member's **own** observations,
then the resulting z is forward-filled onto the 1h grid — *not* z-scored over 30
hourly bars after filling. This is forced, not chosen: a daily series forward-filled
onto 1h bars is piecewise-constant, so 30 *bars* spans only ~1.25 days and the
trailing window is very often perfectly flat ⇒ `std == 0` ⇒ the semantic degeneracy
fence drops the member ⇒ the all-or-nothing composite fence drops nearly every bar.
Z-scoring at the native cadence is also the only reading consistent with V262.md §5's
"forward-fill **at the source cadence**". Concretely: the intraday-native members use
a 30-**bar** window; every daily member uses a 30-**day** window on its own grid;
`yield_curve` is formed as DGS10 − DGS2 on the daily grid before z-scoring; `gdelt` is
the mean of the five `gdelt_tone_*` series' daily z-scores.

**The primary hold is deliberately the friction-hostile choice.** V262.md §7 says
plainly: "do not tune the hold to rescue it." 5 bars is what the inherited mapping
gives, so 5 bars is the primary, and it is the arm the verdict is taken from — even
though a 24 bps round-trip against a 5-hour holding period is the single most likely
thing to kill this version (§7).

**Diagnostic hold ladder (reported, NEVER verdict-bearing):** {5, 24, 72, 168} bars
(5 h / 1 d / 3 d / 7 d). Its purpose is to report the **break-even hold** — the
horizon at which gross edge would cover the declared friction. That is diagnosis of
*where* friction bites, not a second shot at a pass.

**Gross arm (reported, NEVER verdict-bearing):** the identical book at 0 bps. Its
only job is to separate the two REFUTE mechanisms honestly — "there is no edge"
(gross also fails) versus "the edge is real and friction eats it" (gross passes, net
fails). That distinction is what separates REFUTED from FLAG-GATED in §5.

## 5. Falsifiers (from V262.md §6, made numerically concrete)

All three are **hard REFUTE conditions**, evaluated on the **primary arm only**
(hold = 5 bars, 12 bps/side, verdict-bearing universe of 10).

**F1 — pooled median PnL.** REFUTE if pooled median net PnL **≤ $0**, or if the
bootstrap **CI95 does not exclude zero** (10,000 resamples, fixed seed — the V261
`_bootstrap_median_ci` construction).

**F2 — mechanism.** MWU on entry `|composite z|`, winners vs losers. REFUTE if
**p ≥ 0.025** (α Bonferroni-corrected for the 2 arms of §3b) after **N_eff
deflation** (§6). *(The same test that killed V261 at p = 0.942.)*

**F3 — annualized net.** REFUTE if annualized **net** return **< 15%** at the
declared fee model. Expected to be the killer (§7).

### Verdict rule (declared before the run)

| Condition | Verdict |
|---|---|
| F1 + F2 + F3 all pass in the same arm, and the gross/net gap is not the whole story | **ADOPT** |
| F2 passes (mechanism real) **and** the gross arm clears F1/F3, but the **net** arm fails F1 or F3 | **FLAG-GATED** — signal real, fee-eroded at intraday frequency |
| F2 fails, or the gross arm also fails F1/F3 | **REFUTED** |

## 6. N_eff deflation (mandated by F4b)

`V262_F4b_AUTOCORRELATION_VERDICT.md` §7 is explicit: "**quote ~19–21×, never 24×**,
and deflate every F1/F2 significance calculation by N_eff, not N."

| Quantity | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| SLEM deflator `(1−λ₂)/(1+λ₂)` | **0.778** | **The deflator used.** The conservative form, per F4b §4 |
| `1 − λ₂` (the pre-registered form) | 0.872 | Reported alongside for comparison |

**Applied as:** the MWU normal-approximation is computed with effective group sizes
`n_eff = 0.778 × n`, which inflates the standard error by `1/√0.778 ≈ 1.134` and
therefore **widens the p-value** — the conservative direction. The raw (undeflated)
p is reported alongside so the effect of the deflation is visible, but **F2's verdict
uses the deflated p**.

The bootstrap CI95 for F1 is likewise reported in both forms: the plain resample and
a **deflated** resample drawing `round(0.778 × n)` observations per replicate, which
widens the interval. **F1's verdict uses the deflated CI.**

## 7. Expected outcome, stated before the run

**F3 is the most likely killer**, and this is a prediction on the record, not a
retro-fit:

- `V262.md` §7 named transaction-cost erosion the "Highest-probability failure. More
  bars ⇒ more trades ⇒ friction scales linearly while edge does not."
- `V262_AUDIT_VERDICT.md` and both F4 verdicts each independently reaffirm it.
- **The V255.B precedent is decisive:** a *confirmed, real* 36.4% annualized gross
  alpha still died at **−$5.95 median net** once friction met the hold.
- Arithmetic here is harsher than V255.B's: at a 5-hour hold there are ~1,752 holds
  per year, so a 24 bps round-trip is on the order of **~420% of notional per year**
  in friction. Gross edge per 5-hour hold must exceed 24 bps just to break even.

If F3 refutes, **that is the finding** — it is the pre-registered answer to "does
intraday resolution reopen the entry-side composite," and it redirects the campaign
back to spot Victoria plus funding-carry. It is not an invitation to tune the hold.
