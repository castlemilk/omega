## Hypothesis

V207b's static cycle-1 bisect localized the divergence to the
sizing-multiplier chain in `_construct_portfolio`. Per-signal traces,
composites, demeaned composites, conviction scores, and basket stats
are all byte-identical at every shared trade event. The divergence is
a downstream **2× clip-boundary discontinuity** (most likely
`_conv_scale = max(0.5, min(2.0, _w_conv / 0.25))` at lines 3142, 3150,
3173, 3182).

The mechanism: `_compute_weighted_conviction`'s rolling z-score uses
`self._signal_history` keyed on signal-name only, mutated by every
candidate in every cycle. The mean/std at the moment any one symbol
is normalized depends on the iteration order across symbols. A tiny
order-dependent difference in `_w_conv` straddles 0.5 or 1.0 on the
clip, producing exact 2× sizing.

**V208 is methodology-only.** No new feature. The deliverable is a
byte-identical no-op pair on the recent gate, in service of restoring
a trustworthy eval. Walk the four V207b-registered branches in cost
order, stop when the within-pair spread collapses (< $100 with
identical `total_closed`).

## Conclusion

Confirmed. **Iteration order across `(long|short)_candidates` in
`_construct_portfolio` was the third noise channel.** A zero-cost
canonical sort by ticker (plus a ticker tiebreaker on the
`abs(composite)`-descending top-N cut) collapses the within-pair
recent PnL spread from $3,257 to $0.06 with identical trade count.

The mechanism is exactly the one pre-registered in V207b: every
iteration that calls `_compute_weighted_conviction` mutates the
shared per-signal `_signal_history`, and the rolling z-score that
feeds `_w_conv` is therefore order-dependent. Two runs that arrive
at the same `long_candidates` set via different insertion paths
hit different rolling stats and straddle the `max(0.5, min(2.0, …))`
clip → exact-2× sizing divergence at the first clip-boundary symbol.

Why this is sufficient without B/C/D: A is a strict superset of B
in noise-elimination terms — once the iteration order is canonical,
the shared-history pool is still shared, but it's now visited in a
deterministic order on every run, so the z-score is deterministic.
B (per-symbol history) would also kill the channel but at the cost
of a behavioural change to the normalization. C (disable
normalization) is a behavioural change too. A is the only branch
that preserves V207b production semantics byte-for-byte.

The remaining $0.06 spread is consistent with FP-summation noise
(below the $113 V207a crisis residual, indicating no
order-dependent FP path remains on the recent gate). Crisis still
has its own latent FP channel that V207a measured at $113 — that's
a separate phenomenon, not a target for V208.

High-water marks: **not affected** — V208 changes no gate metric,
only the noise floor of the audit harness.
