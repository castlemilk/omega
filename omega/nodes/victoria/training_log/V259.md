## Pre-registration summary (what V259 was going to be)

Track D from V254 scoping: treat **Polymarket crypto binary-option probabilities**
(e.g. "BTC $200k by year-end", "ETH $10k Q4") as a **consumer-sentiment continuous
signal** — a live-feed regime/direction indicator. Pre-declared **$0 offline scorer**:

- **Signal:** rolling z-score(`polymarket_probability`) on a 30d window per major
  crypto binary market.
- **Entry:** `|z| ≥ 1.0` with an additional bet-volume filter.
- **Direction:** LONG spot when probability > mean (bullish crowd), SHORT spot when
  probability < mean (bearish crowd) — **or the sign-flipped inverse** if the crowd is
  systematically wrong ("dumb money"). Both signs pre-declared to be tested against the
  data, no post-hoc sign selection.
- **Sizing:** fixed $10k notional, 5d hold.
- **Falsifier:** Mann-Whitney U p ≥ 0.10, OR pooled median ≤ $0, OR insufficient trades.
- **Cap (pre-declared):** FLAG-GATED — no live Polymarket feed wired into Victoria, so
  even a positive offline result could not ship past a gated flag.

## Why the available Polymarket data fails the pre-registered design

The V259 signal is a **rolling 30d z-score** — it requires, per major crypto binary
market, a **continuous daily (or finer) probability series** spanning at least the 30d
window plus the backtest span. The only Polymarket data on disk is a **single-cycle
snapshot** of vol-arb opportunities: one `yes_price`/`no_price` per market at one
instant. A z-score needs a distribution over time; a single point has no mean and no
std to standardise against. The data is not merely thin — it is **structurally the
wrong shape** (point-in-time, not longitudinal). No amount of the frozen data can
produce the pre-registered signal.

## Anti-Goodhart note — what we are deliberately NOT doing

Tempting salvage: rebuild V259 as a **live-only** scorer that starts accumulating
Polymarket probabilities *now* and z-scores forward. **We are not doing that here.** It
would (a) abandon the pre-registered *offline hermetic* backtest for a live-forward
accrual (a different experiment class — that is the live-paper phase, V250+), and (b)
require wiring a live Polymarket feed into Victoria, which is exactly the FLAG-GATED
integration the pre-registration deferred. If Polymarket-sentiment is ever worth a live
lane, it gets its **own** pre-registration against the live-paper harness, not a quiet
rebranding of the offline V259 into a forward-accrual under time pressure.

Second tempting salvage: reconstruct a probability history by re-querying the
Polymarket CLOB `pricesHistory` endpoint. That is a **data-acquisition task** (a V257-
class freeze pipeline), not something the offline scorer can assume — and it is
explicitly out of scope for an offline-only deliverable with no live-fetch mandate.

## Verdict and reopen criteria

**VERDICT: PAUSED — pending data acquisition. NOT REFUTED.**

The crowd-sentiment thesis is untested, not falsified. The blocker is purely that no
historical Polymarket probability series is frozen. Track D cannot advance offline
until that data exists in hermetic frozen form.

**Reopen criteria:** a data-freeze pipeline (V257-class) delivers a frozen,
byte-identity-checked historical probability series for **at least 3 major crypto
binary markets** (BTC/ETH price-target and macro-crypto questions) with ≥1-year daily

**rerun exactly as pre-registered** (both signs, MWU falsifier, $10k/5d) — a clean
pre-registered test, not a Goodhart salvage. Even a positive result stays **FLAG-GATED**
until a live Polymarket feed is wired (no live feed exists today).
