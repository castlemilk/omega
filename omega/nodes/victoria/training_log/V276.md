## §1 — Hypothesis

V275 flipped `ic_seed_weighting` to `False` and deliberately **declined** to flip
`per_regime_ic_weighting`, because its Phase 0 audit found the premise split: the
second flag is absent from all 44 arms, and in **7 of them it is live and
load-bearing** — including `v274_ic_on_grid.sh:38` and `v274_smoke.sh:32`, the arms
behind V274's own headline result. Flipping it first would have silently redefined
what V274 measured.

The bet:

> Pinning `"per_regime_ic_weighting": true` explicitly into those 7 arms is a
> **no-op that preserves what they measured**. Once pinned, no arm inherits the
> default, so flipping it to `False` is a no-op for every existing arm and a guard
> for every future one — the same structural close V275 achieved for the sibling
> flag, in the only order that does not rewrite history.

**Falsifier (G2):** if any sentinel cell moves by a cent, the pin-then-flip has
surfaced a reliance the audit missed. That is the finding — **STOP and report as
R5**; do not chase the fix in this version.

## §2 — Why this flag's safety argument is NOT V275's

This must be stated plainly, because reusing V275's reasoning here would be wrong.

V275's G2 rested on a **structural** claim: `ic_seed_weighting` has *no
strategy-layer consumer* (`tests/test_features_defaults.py::
test_ic_seed_weighting_has_no_strategy_layer_consumer`), so the flip could not
change strategy behaviour by construction.

**`per_regime_ic_weighting` does not have that property.** It IS read at the
strategy layer:

| Site | Code |
|---|---|
| `strategy.py:1135` | `_per_regime = bool(getattr(self.features, "per_regime_ic_weighting", False))` |
| `strategy.py:1205` | `if _per_regime and self._regime_ics:` |
| `strategy.py:1223` | `if _per_regime and self._regime_ics:` |

So the safety argument is **conditional inertness**, not absence of a consumer, and
it rests on the second conjunct:

- `self._regime_ics` is initialised empty (`strategy.py:488`) and has exactly one
  populate path: `update_regime_ics` (`strategy.py:998`), whose only caller is
  `scripts/run_training.py:1155`.
- That call sits **inside the block gated at `run_training.py:1106`** on
  `bool(getattr(_active_features, "ic_seed_weighting", False))` — verified by
  reading the block bounds, not inferred.

Therefore: with `ic_seed_weighting: false`, `_regime_ics` is `{}`, both guards
short-circuit on the empty dict, and `per_regime_ic_weighting` cannot be observed at
any value. With `ic_seed_weighting: true`, `_regime_ics` is populated and the flag
**is** live — which is precisely why the 7 arms must be pinned first.

This is a weaker guarantee than V275's and is recorded as such. G2 is what tests it
empirically.

## §7 — Conclusion

**The bet held: pin-then-flip is a $0.00 no-op on both arms, and the ordering was the
whole point.** `per_regime_ic_weighting` now defaults `False`, all 7 arms that were
live under it state their value explicitly, and V274's own ARM_ON still measures what
V274 measured — proved, not asserted, by G2b's paired control.

V275 closed `ic_seed_weighting` with a *structural* argument (no strategy-layer
consumer). That argument does not exist for this flag: it **is** read at
`strategy.py:1135`. V276's safety is *conditional* — `_regime_ics` is empty under
IC-OFF because its one populate path sits inside the `ic_seed_weighting` gate — and
that condition is now pinned by `test_regime_ics_only_populated_under_ic_seed_weighting`
rather than left to a future reader to re-derive. A weaker guarantee, stated as weaker,
and tested empirically instead of argued.

**The unplanned finding is the more important one.** V276's control was run only to
attribute a delta, and it revealed that the standing baseline — crisis +$599 / trend
+$2,997 / recent +$30 — does **not** reproduce on this machine: two of three sentinels
are off by −$67 and +$53 with trade counts identical. V274 certified that baseline's
*data* provenance (32/32 cells IC-OFF, 0.000000 re-run drift) and V275 restored its
*substrate*, but both were measured on one host. The measuring stick is reproducible
with respect to the frozen inputs and **not** with respect to the code environment.

That is a gap in the campaign's foundations, not in V276, and it deserves the same
treatment `data/.cache_manifest.json` gave the frozen inputs.

**Standing baseline: UNCHANGED and NOT edited.** `data/standing_baseline.json` was
not touched. No high-water claim is made; no number here is a performance result.
