## S1 — The calendar constraint (Phase 0, `V249_MANIFEST_AUDIT.md`)

The V247 ruler's α sub-path — "widen the walk-forward manifest to add independent
recent windows, lowering the 2·SE bar" — was the last live lever after V248. Phase 0
audited whether that widening is executable from existing frozen data. It is not,
for recent:

- The primary 90d/90d grid **already tiles the whole span** 2020-01-01 → 2026-06-30
  with **zero calendar gaps**. Theoretical non-overlapping 90d slots in the span:
  **26**. Primary windows already in the manifest: **26**. Every independent 90d
  calendar slot is already used.
- Of those 26 independent slots the mechanical regime classifier
  (`walk_forward_freeze.py:regime_label`, zero discretion) assigns only **4 to
  recent** (`20210922, 20230316, 20250305, 20260228`). The nominal "recent n=10"
  is **4 independent + 6 overlapping ±45d offset supplements** — the offsets each
  overlap two primary neighbours by 45d, i.e. literal data reuse, not new
  information.
- **Independent recent windows harvestable for free: 0.**
  **Independent windows of any regime harvestable for free: 0.**
- Doubling recent N 10 → 20 (the premise that would have made V248's +$494 and
  V243-A's +$232 near-misses adjudicable) is **calendar-infeasible**. The span
  contains no more independent recent stretches at 90d granularity, at any cost
  short of waiting for future recent-regime months to elapse.

Why recent is structurally scarce (not a labelling bug): at 90d granularity the
30%-max-drawdown crisis bar is hit often (crypto 90d windows routinely draw down
≥30% even in flat tape), so "recent/chop" survives only in the 4 calmest
independent 90d stretches of the entire 6.5-year span. This is a property of
regime-definition × window-length × asset-volatility, not of the data pipeline.

Denser offsets (30d/15d) could raise *nominal* recent to ~13–15 but every added
window overlaps ≥67% — effective independent recent barely moves off 4. Shorter
windows (45–60d) are infeasible (`ReplayIngestionNode` 30-bar warmup + the grid's
`--cycles = min_bars−31` cap leave ~15 honest cycles, below hold length → trades
never close). Span extension beyond 2026-06-30 yields 0 complete windows today
(last window ends 2026-05-29; the next 90d slot needs data through late August).

**The one executable widening — and why it doesn't rescue recent:** refetch the 20
non-recent offset windows (OHLCV re-fetch was empirically probed this session and
works — Binance klines returned, so no geo-block STOP). That grows **pooled** N
32 → 52, sharpening the pooled MDE ~$875 → ~$750. But it leaves **recent** N and
recent MDE ($1,043) **unchanged**, flips **zero** near-misses to ADOPT (V248's
+$494 stays below even the tightened pooled bar), and permanently raises every
future grid's cost ~60% (52 vs 32 OFF-arm cells). **Decision: do NOT refetch.**
The pooled instrument is not the binding constraint; recent is, and refetching
cannot touch recent.

## S2 — What this means: recent alpha is structurally *unadjudicable*, not absent

The careful statement — the one this whole doc exists to make precise:

> **This is "no adjudication," NOT "no alpha."**

V248's regime-conditional exits scored pooled mean-Δ **+$494**; V246's global
exit adaptivity scored **+$627**; V243-A's blacklist extension scored recent
mean-Δ **+$232**. Every one of these is *positive*. Every one *might be real*.
We cannot tell, because the recent-regime noise floor (2·SE ≈ $727–$1,043 at
n=4-effective / n=10-nominal) sits above the effect sizes we are trying to
resolve. The bar is not too strict — the bar is honest (V247 derived it from
the variance table). The data is too thin.

The V241→V248 streak reads as "8 mechanisms failed." Corrected: **the objective
became unfalsifiable, and the ruler (V247) is what let us see that.** V248 is the
proof the ruler works — it caught a genuinely-interesting +$494 near-miss and
spent **$0 grid** declining it, instead of the full grid V246 burned to reach an
equivalent verdict. Continuing to draw mechanisms against a sub-resolution
objective is not science; it is variance mining. The loop has correctly run out
of the thing it was designed to consume: independent recent windows.

## S5 — Recommended next phase: live PAPER trading (simulated PnL, real market data)

**Not** live-broker execution. **Not** moving money. The recommendation is a
**live paper-trading harness**: real, forward, streaming market data → the frozen
strategy's decisions → **simulated** PnL bookkeeping, no orders, no funds, no
broker.

Why this is the correct phase transition and not just "keep grinding":

- The one thing the loop is out of is **independent recent windows**, and the one
  thing that manufactures them is **the passage of time**. Every 90d that elapses
  forward is exactly **1 new independent window**, correctly regime-labelled by
  the same mechanical classifier, with **zero overlap** with anything in the
  frozen manifest.
- Recent independent N grows at **~1 per quarter** by the calendar alone — the
  rate the frozen data can never supply. In ~4 quarters the recent instrument
  roughly doubles its independent N; the V247 α widening that was infeasible from
  disk becomes *free* from the clock.
- Paper trading also closes the one honesty gap the backtest cannot: the frozen
  windows are all *in-sample to the strategy's design history*. Forward paper PnL
  is genuinely out-of-sample — the first truly un-Goodhartable measurement the
  campaign will have.

Paper, explicitly, because the campaign's standing guardrail is **never execute a
trade / move money**. The harness observes and simulates; it never touches a
broker. This section makes **no live-broker-execution recommendation of any
kind.**
