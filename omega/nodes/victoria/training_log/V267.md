## 1. Why this version exists

V266's load-bearing caveat, recorded before any V267 analysis ran:

> **Capacity, not Sharpe, is funding-carry's binding constraint.** V255.C runs
> ~$2k–$10k notional per trade (median $3,266.67), median +$1.95/trade, 1,225
> in-range trades, Sharpe 2.20 at that size.

Every downstream campaign move — widening recent-N on funding-carry, promoting
live-paper toward real, any portfolio sizing decision — is gated on one unanswered
question: **does the edge survive at a size worth trading?** A Sharpe-2.20 lane that
tops out at $10k of notional is a curiosity, not an allocation.

This is a **$0 analysis on committed ledgers plus already-frozen Binance market
data**. No new grids, no compute burn, no network.

## 2. Scope exclusion declared UP FRONT (not a result — a data audit)

The brief's suggested G1/G2 scaffold was *"fit a market-impact model from V255.C
fills (or orderbook snapshots) and report Sharpe(2k / 20k / 200k / 2M)"*. **That is
not answerable from data on disk, and this document says so before running
anything:**

| Required input | On disk? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Orderbook depth / L2 snapshots | **NO** | `frozen_series/` holds only klines, `fundingRate`, `metrics` (OI + ratios), `markPriceKlines`, `indexPriceKlines` |
| Own fills with observed slippage | **NO** | `v255c_trades.csv` has no fill/slippage column; the scorer charges a **flat** 5 bps/side maker fee (`params.fee_bps_per_side = 5.0`) |
| Fills above $10k notional | **NO** | `notional_distribution.max = 10000.0`; 244 trades already at the cap |

There is therefore **zero variation in executed size against which an impact
coefficient could be estimated**. Producing a Sharpe(200k) / Sharpe(2M) curve would
require assuming a square-root-law coefficient from the literature and then
reporting the assumption's output as a measurement. **That is fabrication with a
citation, and this campaign does not do it.** The fitted-impact-curve lane is
recorded as **R4 (data-blocked)** in §6 and is not scored.

What *is* on disk is real and sufficient for a different, honest capacity question:

- **1h klines, 2020-01 → 2026-06, all 13 ledger symbols** → true daily quote volume
  (ADV) at every trade's entry date.
- **Binance futures `metrics` archive, 5-minute `sum_open_interest_value` (USD)**,
  BTCUSDT from 2020-09-01, alts from their listing → true open interest at entry.

So V267 measures the **capacity envelope** (how big can the book get before it is a
material share of the market it trades) and the **slippage budget** (how much
execution cost the measured edge can absorb before it dies), and it tests the one
capacity hypothesis that *is* falsifiable from the ledger: **is the edge a liquidity
premium?**

## 3. Hypotheses

- **H1** — The book can be scaled ≥100× per-trade notional while staying under
  standard no-material-impact participation thresholds.
- **H2** — The measured per-trade edge can absorb ≥5 bps of additional per-leg
  round-trip slippage and still clear Sharpe 1.0.
- **H3** — The edge is **not** a liquidity premium: it survives restriction to the
  most-liquid tercile of trade-days, including in the `recent` regime where V266
  measured Sharpe +2.06.

## 4. Pre-registered gates (LOCKED — no post-hoc tuning)

**G0 — Phase-0 data coverage (a gate, not a formality).**

| Join | Bar |
|---|---|
| trades joined to real entry-date ADV | **≥ 80%** of 1,225 |
| trades joined to real entry-date OI | **≥ 60%** of 1,225 |

If a join falls below bar, the gates depending on it are reported **R4 / data-blocked
and NOT scored**. **No imputation, no forward-fill across symbols, no synthetic
volume.** Unjoined trades are dropped and counted, never filled.

| Gate | Statistic | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| **G1 — capacity envelope** | at scale k=100× per-trade notional: median participation vs entry-date ADV **and** vs entry-date OI | **≤ 1.00% of ADV** AND **≤ 0.50% of OI** |
| **G2 — slippage budget** | extra per-leg round-trip slippage `s` (bps of notional, charged on 2 legs × entry+exit) at which annualised Sharpe of the daily book PnL falls to 1.0 | **s\* ≥ 5.0 bps** |
| **G3 — liquidity-conditioned edge** | top-ADV-tercile trades: median edge (bps of notional) vs pooled median edge; 10k bootstrap CI; `recent`-regime Sharpe within that tercile | median ≥ **50%** of pooled AND **CI95 excludes 0** AND recent-regime Sharpe **> 1.0** |

**Thresholds are stated assumptions, not fitted quantities.** 1% of ADV and 0.5% of
OI are the conventional "no material impact" participation heuristics; they are
declared here so the reader can substitute their own — the scorer emits the full
participation distribution at k ∈ {1, 10, 100, 1000} and the **maximum k at which
both thresholds hold**, which is the actual deliverable number.

**Outcome mapping:**

- **ADOPT** — G1+G2+G3 all pass. Capacity is not the binding constraint at the
  tested scale; funding-carry is promotable and a V267-2 sizing/allocation
  pre-registration is queued.
- **CAVEATED** — some pass. State exactly which capacity dimension binds.
- **STOP** — none pass. The lane is size-bound; V266's "Sharpe 2.20" is a
  small-notional artifact and the campaign's single confirmed alpha does not scale.

## 5. Method (fixed in advance)

1. **Ledger.** V255.D-EXT **frozen** real-basis ledger
   (`$AUDIT/v255_D_ext/frozen/v255c_trades.csv`, 1,225 trades, 13 names,
   2020-01-31 → 2026-05-07). This is the same artifact V266 used, unmodified.
2. **ADV join.** For each `(symbol, entry_date)`: sum column 8 (`quote_asset_volume`)
   over the 24 1h bars of that UTC date from
   `frozen_series/binance_intraday_raw/{SYM}/1h/{monthly,daily}`. **Timestamp units
   vary between ms (13-digit) and µs (16-digit) across archive eras — detect per row
   by magnitude** (the V262 P0 determinism defect; do not assume).
3. **OI join.** For each `(symbol, entry_date)`: mean `sum_open_interest_value` over
   that date's 5-minute rows from
   `frozen_series/binance_futures/{SYM}/daily/metrics/`. Real values only.
4. **G1.** participation_ADV = k · notional / ADV; participation_OI = k · notional /
   OI. Report median / p75 / p95 at k ∈ {1, 10, 100, 1000}, plus max feasible k.
   Also report **peak concurrent gross book notional** (7-day holds overlap, so the
   per-trade figure understates the book) at each k.
5. **G2.** Per-trade edge_bps = 1e4 · pnl_usd / notional_usd. Extra cost at slippage
   `s` = notional · (s/1e4) · **4** (2 legs × entry+exit). Aggregate to daily book
   PnL by `exit_date`; annualised Sharpe = mean/σ · √365 (crypto trades every
   calendar day — same convention as V266). Solve for `s*` at Sharpe = 1.0 and for
   `s_0` at pooled median net = 0. 10k bootstrap CI, **seed 42**.
6. **G3.** Tercile trades by entry-date ADV (and, as a robustness read, by OI).
   Compare median edge_bps and daily Sharpe across terciles. Map each trade's
   `exit_date` into the **26 independent primary** walk-forward windows
   (`data/walk_forward_manifest.json`; the 6 `_recent_supplements` overlap by 45d and
   are excluded — V249 independent-N rule) for the crisis/trend/recent decomposition.
7. **Verdict.**

## 8. What would convince us the bet was wrong

If G1 shows the book saturates 1% of ADV below k=100 (i.e. under ~$330k per trade /
low-single-digit $M gross), or G3 shows the edge lives in the illiquid tercile and
vanishes in the liquid one, then funding-carry is a **small-book** strategy. That is
a campaign-level finding: the single confirmed alpha would be real, out-of-sample,
uncorrelated — and economically irrelevant at any size worth the operational risk of
going live. The honest response in that case is STOP and re-aim, not a V267-2.
