## Hypothesis (pre-registered)

At a **longer hold (7 days)** + **level-proportional sizing** + **realistic maker
fees**, the confirmed 36% gross carry translates to **positive net pooled median
PnL**, with the p ≈ 0 |entry funding| separator preserved.

Mechanism (unchanged from V255.B except the three levers): being the carry receiver
(short-perp+long-spot when funding > 0; long-perp+short-spot when funding < 0)
collects funding; the spot leg hedges the perp price risk. The fixed 4-fill fee is
paid once per round trip regardless of hold, so a longer hold accrues more carry
against the same fee. Level-scaled sizing puts more notional where |funding| (and
hence per-day carry) is large relative to the fee.

## Pre-declared parameters (do NOT tune post-hoc)

| parameter | V255.C value | change vs V255.B | rationale (fixed here, before running) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hold period** | **7 days** | 3 → 7 | amortizes the fixed 4-fill fee over more carry accrual; 7d = natural cadence for weekly funding-regime persistence (V255 regime analysis). |
| **Position sizing** | **notional = min($10k, $10k × \|funding_bps\| / 5)** per leg | fixed $10k → level-scaled | $10k baseline at 5 bps/8h funding, scales *down* proportionally for smaller funding; Kelly-lite proxy — harvest more where carry ≫ fee, less at marginal funding. |
| **Fee model** | **single-leg 5 bps maker per side, 4 fills = 20 bps round-trip** on notional | unchanged (same rate) | standard exchange maker fee tier; taker (10 bps/side) already refuted in V255.B. Recorded with an explicit `maker=True` flag. |
| **Entry threshold** | **\|funding\| ≥ 0.0001** (0.01% per 8h) | unchanged | same as V255.B, pre-declared. |
| **Regime filter** | **exclude `near_zero`**; keep {negative_carry, positive_carry, high_vol} | unchanged | same as V255.B, pre-declared. |

**Sizing units.** The frozen series stores the daily mean of the 3 per-8h
settlements (per-8h units). `funding_bps = |entry funding| × 10_000`. At the pre-reg
reference funding of 5 bps/8h (`entry funding = 0.0005`), `funding_bps = 5` →
notional = `min($10k, $10k × 5/5) = $10k`. At the entry threshold (1 bps/8h,
`0.0001`) → notional = `$10k × 1/5 = $2k`. Notional is capped at $10k and floored
implicitly by the entry threshold.

**Fee independence of notional.** Fee = `notional × (n_legs × 2 × fee_bps_side)/1e4`
= `notional × 0.002`. Because both the fee and every PnL component scale linearly
with notional, the **sign** of each trade's net PnL is independent of its size
(notional > 0). Level-scaled sizing therefore changes mean/total PnL (concentrating
capital in high-carry trades) but **cannot flip the sign of the pooled median** —
the median sign is governed purely by whether the pooled win rate exceeds 50%. This
is stated up front so the median falsifier cannot be gamed by a sizing choice: the
7-day hold is the only lever that can move win rate, and it does so honestly by
letting more trades' carry clear the fixed fee.

## Basis-cleanliness caveat — CARRIED FORWARD from V255.B (mandatory, pre-caps verdict)

mark / spot index). The spot and perp price legs are computed from the same series
and cancel to **exactly $0 by construction, not by measurement**. Therefore:

- The "basis-hedge fails empirically" falsifier **cannot fire** (residual is
