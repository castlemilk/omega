## 0. What this version is, and what it explicitly is NOT

V272 found one lookahead defect and showed it was load-bearing:
`FundingRegimeClassifier._standardize` (`omega/nodes/funding_carry/regime.py`)
computes `(x - mean(all_x)) / std(all_x)` over the **full span**, and the
labels it produces gate entry decisions. Repaired causally (expanding window),
V255.C's median per-trade edge collapses **$1.9539 → $0.3392**, statistically
indistinguishable from dropping the filter entirely ($0.2950).

V273 is the **pattern-generalization move**: one lookahead found, look for its
siblings before adopting anything else.

- **IS** a categorized inventory of every place in `omega/` (and `scripts/` for
  the V-series offline scorers) where a scorer-time statistic could feed an
  entry-time decision.
- **IS NOT** a fix. No `regime.py` edit, no strategy code, no `omega/nodes/`

- **IS NOT** a score. No run, no grid, no verdict on any specific alpha. No

- **IS NOT** a queue dispatch. This document IS the queue; triage is the user's.

## 1. The defect signature (pre-registered, before searching)

A site is an instance of the **V272 class** iff all three hold:

- **(i) Non-prefix read.** The function computes a statistic over more than the
  prefix of its input series — i.e. the value emitted at index `i` depends on
  `x[j]` for some `j > i`. Full-span `mean`/`std`/`quantile`/`rank`/`min`/`max`,
  a fitted scaler applied to the same data it was fit on, or any centered/
  two-sided window all qualify.
- **(ii) Entry-time consumption.** The output is consumed — directly or through
  N call frames — by code that decides whether/what/how much to trade. The
  operative test is the V272 one: *would this backtest-scorer become a
  forward-scorer unchanged?* If yes, and the statistic is non-prefix, the rule
  is not runnable forward.
- **(iii) No causal-window bound.** There is no `rolling(`, expanding prefix
  slice, `[:i]`, warmup fence, or equivalent that restricts the read to the past.

### False-positive shapes, excluded explicitly

Named here **before** searching, so the exclusions can't be motivated after the
fact:

1. **Model-training normalization.** A scaler fit on a training split and
   applied within that split during fitting (batch-norm, `StandardScaler` in a
   `fit` path). Only becomes a defect if the *same* fitted object is then used
   to produce labels/features for out-of-sample or forward decisions, or if the
   fit spans the eval period.
2. **Evaluation-only metrics.** Sharpe, IC, drawdown, p25, PnL aggregation,
   bootstrap CIs — statistics computed *about* a completed ledger. These read
   the full span by construction and that is their job.
3. **Reporting aggregations.** Dashboard/API/summary numbers that never re-enter
   a trading path.
4. **Gate/ruler evaluation.** `omega/eval/*` is post-run by construction
   (`standing_gates.py`, `grid_ruler.py`). Full-span reads there are correct
   *unless* a gate output feeds back into sizing/selection — which is checked,
   not assumed.
5. **Cross-validation / held-out splits.** Splitting is not leakage; leakage is
   fitting across the split.
6. **Config/constant derivation** computed once from a frozen historical corpus
   that pre-dates the eval span, where the boundary is pre-registered
   (the fixed `_LEVEL_HI = 0.5` style constants are NOT the defect — the
   *standardization feeding them* is).

An excluded site is recorded as **N/A** with its reason, not silently dropped.

### 2b. Targeted structural surfaces

- Regime classifiers: `omega/nodes/**/regime*.py`, `omega/core/**/regime*.py`,
  `omega/**/classifier*.py`, `omega/**/detector*.py`
- Custom aggregators: any function taking a series and returning a
  series-length label/score (the V272 shape exactly)
- The V-series offline scorers under `scripts/` (`v2*_scorer.py`,
  `v2*_score*.py`, `run_*_scorer*.py`) — these are the files whose verdicts the

- Signal modules under `omega/nodes/victoria/signals/` and
  `omega/nodes/funding_carry/`
- `training_log/*.md` entries citing "walk-forward" or "OOS" whose numbers may
  rest on a full-span helper

### 2c. Known coverage gaps (declared up front)

The grep is idiom-based and will miss:

- Hand-rolled loops that accumulate a total then divide (`s = 0; for x in xs:
  s += x` … `s/len(xs)`) with no `mean` token
- Full-span reads via `max(`/`min(`/`sorted(` used as a normalizer
  (partially covered by 2b's manual read, not by 2a)
- Leakage through a *cached artifact* — a frozen file built with future data
  and then read causally-looking at runtime (`data/*.json`, `macro_cache.db`)
- Cross-sectional (same-timestamp, across-symbol) statistics, which are
  **not** lookahead but look identical to grep. Distinguished by reading.
- Non-Python surfaces (Go `internal/`, SQL) — out of scope this pass.

A future audit should extend along these axes.

## 8. Campaign implication

The audit finds that Victoria's **backtest substrate is sound** — the replay
cursor, the V238 series provider and the whole funding-carry simulator are
prefix-clean, and two of the campaign's regime labellers (`walk_forward_freeze.
regime_label`, `v262_f4b.label_windows`) are the shape `funding_carry/regime.py`
should have been. The leaks are all in the **layer that bypasses the substrate**:
retrospectively-fitted state that is loaded at startup, and one cache that
resolves against its own newest row instead of the replayed bar. Concretely,
the verdicts to flag as *may not survive causal audit* are: **(1) every
V222→V229 IC-overlay result and, more importantly, the standing baseline itself**
— `ic_seed_weighting` and `per_regime_ic_weighting` are **defaults-ON** (H3), so
crisis +$599 / trend +$2,997 / recent +$30 were all produced with
retrospectively-derived IC weights in the conviction filter; the IC-OFF control
is one flag away and this is the highest-value single re-score in the queue.
**(2) Any window whose result depended on `dxy_signal` or `yield_curve`** (H2),
bounded but not excused by V240.B's near-inert measurement — the honest statement
is that four years of macro lookahead were structurally available and measured to
contribute little, which is a *finding about effect size*, not about correctness.
**(3) V266's tangency composition** (H6) — the 8/92 hairline pass is in-sample and
should not be cited as a validated mix; V266's own G2 gate, keyed on the a-priori
50/50, is unaffected. **(4) V244** (H5) is shape-affected but its verdict was
already a $0 refutation, so nothing banked moves. Verdicts that are **not**
implicated and should be recorded as surviving: V270's spread budget, V269's
depth acquisition, V262's intraday F1–F4, V261's on-chain refutation, V231's
distributional finding, and the V247 grid ruler — all post-run measurement over
completed ledgers, exactly the exclusion this audit pre-registered. V255.C/D
remain adjudicated by V272 and are not re-opened here.
