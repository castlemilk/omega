## A. Alpha thesis for funding carry

Perpetual futures pay/receive funding every 8h (Binance: 3 settlements/day) to
tether the perp price to spot. When funding is **positive**, longs pay shorts;
systematically **being short the perp collects that carry**. Historically funding
runs +1 bp/settlement on the majors (measured: BTC daily-mean funding +0.000109,
ETH +0.000130 over 2020→2026 — ~+8–14% annualized if harvested continuously).

Two forms of the strategy:
- **v1 basis (hedged):** short-perp **+ long-spot** when funding positive. Price
  risk is hedged out; you keep the funding carry. Net-long-carry, low directional
  exposure. Requires two legs (spot + perp) and borrow.
- **v2 directional (unhedged):** short-perp when funding *very* positive, long-perp
  when *very* negative — a directional bet on funding **mean-reversion**. One leg,
  simpler, but carries full price risk.

**Regime-independence from spot.** Funding is driven by leverage demand and
basis-trade positioning — market *structure*, not spot direction. This is the
whole point: a funding strategy's PnL clock is decoupled from the spot
crisis/trend/recent clock, so it supplies independent windows.

**Refutation-vulnerability check vs V241–V248 patterns:**
- **Not** a signal-on-existing-composite bet (V241–V245 all died there) — it's a
  standalone book with its own PnL, not a feature into Victoria's composite.
- **Not** a portfolio-sizing-only bet (V243 died there).
- **Not** an exit-adaptation bet (V246/V248 died there).
- It is a **fundamentally different alpha source** (funding, not momentum) with
  different regime structure. That independence is *why* V254 ranked it #1 — and
  it is exactly what Phase 0 tested honestly rather than assumed.

## E. Regime structure (the key differentiator)

Funding regimes are classified from a **market-wide funding index** (cross-
sectional mean daily funding) via its 30d trailing level + volatility, with
**pre-registered fixed boundaries** (`regime.py`: level ±0.5σ, vol +1.0σ) — the
classifier is defined *before* and *independent of* any trade outcome
(anti-Goodhart). Regimes: `positive_carry` / `negative_carry` / `near_zero` /
`high_vol`.

**Independent-window count (V249 preflight applied to funding):** tiling the
2341-day span into 26 non-overlapping 90d slots, majority-labelled by funding
regime → `{near_zero: 16, negative_carry: 7, high_vol: 3, positive_carry: 0}`.

This is a **different partition** than spot (crisis 12 / trend 10 / recent 4) —
so funding windows *are* structurally independent of spot windows (different
clock, thesis holds). **But it is not a windfall of new N:** `near_zero` swamps
the count and no slot is majority `positive_carry`. A carry book earning only in
genuine funding regimes has an effective independent-N ≈ 7+3 = 10 — comparable
to spot, not dramatically larger. The "genuinely independent" claim survives;
the "much more N" claim does not clearly hold for this classifier.

## Conclusion — Phase 0: **v2 directional REFUTED**

Full results in [`V255_PHASE0.md`](V255_PHASE0.md). Headline:

| gate check | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| MWU two-sided p ≤ 0.10 | **p = 0.6068** (scipy-confirmed) | ✗ |
| pooled median trade > $0 | **−$44.06** | ✗ |

**Entry z-score does not discriminate winners from losers** (winners' |z| 1.412
≈ losers' 1.442). The strategy loses −$260k over 2300 trades. Per the
pre-registered gate: **REFUTED.**

**Why (the useful part):** decomposition shows the loss is entirely **price
risk** (price_ret −$292k), not the carry thesis — the **funding component is
positive as §A predicted (+$55k, +$24/trade)**. The v2 directional form is a
momentum-contrarian bet (short the assets rallying hardest) that the funding
carry can't pay for. Two clean, non-Goodhart secondary findings direct the
follow-on:
1. The correct entry variable is funding **level**, not z-score (hedged-carry
   separator on |entry funding| is **p < 0.0001**).
2. The loss is **entirely** in the `near_zero` funding regime (58% of trades,
   >100% of the loss); in real funding regimes v2 trades are median-positive
   with WR > 50%.

But the honest magnitude ceiling: hedged basis carry (v1) is only ~7–10%
annualized **gross, median ≈ $0, tail-driven**, and *before* real basis-execution
frictions (borrow, two-leg slippage, tracking). It does not clear a "reliably
profitable" bar at this crude parametrization.

**High-water table:** unchanged. Funding carry is a *new parallel book* with no
Victoria high-water to move; the spot baseline (crisis +$599 / trend +$2,997 /
recent +$30) stands untouched. This is a $0 Phase-0 refutation, exactly the fast
adjudication V254 promised for this lane.
