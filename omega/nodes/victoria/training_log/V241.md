## Hypothesis (one sentence)

Turning `reasoning_layer_enabled` ON — the Kimi v2.7 per-cycle basket review
served from the committed frozen LLM cache — improves recent expectancy without
breaking trend/crisis, measured as a full 32-window walk-forward distribution
against the V240/V239 standing baseline.

## Pre-committed structure

- **Grid:** 32 windows × {reasoning OFF, reasoning ON} × N=1 sleep=0 = 64 cells
  on gamma, `OMEGA_AUDIT_OUTPUT_DIR` on gamma, reasoning served entirely from
  `data/frozen_llm_cache/` under `OMEGA_FROZEN_CACHE=1` (no live calls in the
  grid — the cache is pre-filled in V240's cache-fill pass and committed).
- **OFF cells must be byte-identical** to the V240/V239 standing baseline (the
  same OFF-identity contract as every version since V214).
- Every cell's determinism gate must PASS $0.00 (LLM-in-loop determinism was
  certified in V240; the grid re-certifies per cell).
- Baseline: whatever V239 adopts as standing (universe-flipped if adopted, else
  the V238 baseline).

## Falsifier (pre-registered — the version SHIPS ON only if ALL hold)

Reasoning ON adopted as standing **iff**, pooled over the walk-forward grid:

1. **recent mean-Δ > +$400** (the layer must earn its keep in the worst regime —
   recent is the standing target since V235), AND
2. **recent p25-Δ > +$500** (tail improvement, not just a mean pulled by one
   window), AND
3. **trend mean-Δ > −$300 AND crisis mean-Δ > −$300** (no regime regresses — the
   information/reasoning must not cost the regimes that already work).

Noise context: recent 2·SE ≈ $2,400 (REFLECTION_V237 §2) — a +$400 mean-Δ is
inside noise on its own, which is why the falsifier **conjoins** a tail bar
(p25) and no-regression bars rather than resting on the recent mean alone. If
the conjunction holds, the effect is coherent across mean+tail+other-regimes; if
any clause fails, the layer stays flag-gated OFF and per-cycle forensics decide
whether a narrower trigger (e.g. reasoning only in recent, or only as a veto
never an amplifier) is worth a follow-up.

## FAIL modes

- Determinism FAIL ⇒ bisect/fence per the standard arc before any verdict (an
  LLM cache-hit that is not byte-stable means the pin/temperature contract broke
  — fix in V240 territory, do not paper over in the grid).
- Cache miss under frozen mode ⇒ `LLMCacheMiss` aborts the cell; the cache-fill
  coverage was incomplete — re-fill and re-commit, never serve a neutral stub.

## Explicitly out of scope

Tuning the layer's keep/drop/size bounds or the retrieval index on the eval set
(that would be fitting the falsifier); live trading; any model other than the
V240-pinned Kimi v2.7.
