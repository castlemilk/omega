## Hypothesis

The 2024aug loss is a **selection** failure, not a sizing or conviction-composition failure.
The model enters correctly-identified-in-hindsight *losing* shorts on a broad correlated
grind-down because nothing at the **candidate-selection** layer vetoes a short whose edge is
about to mean-revert. If we add an **entry-time discriminator** — a signal that is
informative at the moment of entry, not lagging — that either (a) vetoes the pre-reversal
shorts or (b) flips/downweights them, the 2024aug **trade ledger will change** (Δtrades ≠ 0,
the bar V234 never cleared) and the loss will shrink, without regressing 2020q1 (+$15.5k) or
2022h1.
