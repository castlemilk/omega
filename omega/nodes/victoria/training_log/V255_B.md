## Hypothesis + mechanism

A **level-entry, near_zero-excluded, basis-hedged** carry harvester earns positive
net carry after friction. Mechanism:

- Perp funding is paid/received every 8h to tether perp to spot. Being the **carry
  receiver** (short perp when funding > 0; long perp when funding < 0) collects
  funding. **Hedging the price leg with an opposite spot position** removes the
  directional price risk that killed v2, isolating the carry.
- Enter only when |funding| is **structurally elevated** (a *level* signal), and
  only outside `near_zero` (where Phase 0 showed there is no carry to harvest and
  the trade degenerates to price-noise-fighting).

## Basis-hedge implementation & the empirical basis check

`funding_carry/basis_hedge.py` models spot + perp concurrently. On the frozen
data the price legs are computed from the **same** close series (see limitation
below), so:

- **price PnL = spot_long_ret + perp_short_ret = 0 by construction** (single
  series ⇒ the two legs cancel algebraically).
- Surviving PnL per trade = `notional × (Σ funding·3 over hold − 2-leg fee)`.

### DATA LIMITATION discovered at preflight (surfaced BEFORE the scorer)

The frozen data contains **exactly one `close` price series per symbol** (stitched
OHLCV). There is **no separate perp-mark and spot-index series** anywhere on disk
(`data/frozen_series/` holds only funding / OI / taker-ratio; snapshots hold one
`close`). Consequences, stated honestly up front:

1. The task's literal empirical basis check — "compute realized basis (perp −
   spot)" — **cannot be performed**: there is no second series to difference.
2. With one series, the hedge cancels price risk **by construction, not by
   measurement**. The pre-registered falsifier "spot+perp PnL sum doesn't cancel
   the price risk" therefore **cannot fire** (the sum is identically 0).
3. This means V255.B measures **carry-under-a-perfect-hedge**. The
   basis-cleanliness assumption (zero basis slippage, pre-declared in the task)
   is **UNTESTED on this data** — a hard caveat that **caps any positive verdict
   at KEEP-FLAG-GATED** and makes real-basis-execution measurement a **mandatory
   gate for any V255.C**.

Best-available proxy reported in Phase 2: the funding rate itself is the market's
clamped price of the perp premium, so its distribution bounds the *level* of the
implied basis; but the hedge's residual (Δbasis over the hold) remains
unmeasurable here.
