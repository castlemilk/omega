## 1. Why this version exists

V264's own finding, verbatim from its §3a: *"Fine-tuning taught Kronos our
volatility, not our direction."* The fine-tuned model emitted higher-dispersion
paths, which nudged rank correlation up (+0.028 → +0.047, still under the F4 bar)
and made point-forecast RMSE decisively worse (0/8 cells beat a random walk, and
every cell got worse than zero-shot).

Both V263 and V264 flagged **distributional** use as the one Kronos idea the
campaign has not tested, and both placed it in the regime/sizing lane rather than
the entry-signal lane. V264 §5 listed it as reopener (1), the highest-prior
untested item, explicitly noting *"a volatility/uncertainty estimate feeding the
regime layer needs no directional edge to be useful."*

V265 tests exactly that claim and closes the question either way.

## 2. Hypothesis

> The **standard deviation across Kronos's `sample_count=16` forecast paths**
> predicts realized N-hour volatility better than a naive baseline (rolling std
> of past returns).

If yes, Kronos becomes a legitimate regime/sizing input for the two standing
lanes (spot Victoria, funding-carry). If no, distributional use also closes and
the foundation-model direction is fully exhausted.

## 3. Estimators (locked with the gates)

Let `C_0` be the last actual close of the 400-bar lookback and `H` the horizon.

| Quantity | Definition |
|---|---|
| realized `σ_real` | `sqrt( mean_{t=1..H} r_t² )`, `r_t = log(C_t / C_{t-1})` |
| kronos `σ̂` | `mean_{t=1..H} ( std_s[ log(C_{s,t}/C_0) ] / sqrt(t) )` |
| naive `σ_nv` | `sqrt( mean of the last 24 squared per-bar log returns of the lookback )` |

All three are **per-bar volatilities in log-return units**, so they are directly
comparable. Notes on the two non-obvious choices:

- `σ_real` is the RMS (zero-mean) realized-vol estimator rather than a sample
  standard deviation, because a sample std is undefined at `H = 1` and three of
  the eight cells are h1.
- `σ̂` is the brief's "mean std over horizon" put in per-bar units: raw
  cross-sample dispersion grows like `sqrt(t)` under a diffusion, so each
  per-timestep dispersion is divided by `sqrt(t)` before averaging. Cross-sample
  dispersion uses `ddof=1` (unbiased at finite S = 16).

## 4. Pre-registered gates — LOCKED

Pooling convention is V264's: **mean across the eight cells**, so no cell
dominates by virtue of a larger price scale or a longer horizon.

| Gate | Statistic | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| **F5-vol** | pooled `RMSE(σ̂, σ_real) / RMSE(σ_nv, σ_real)` | **< 0.90** |
| **F5-corr** | pooled Spearman `ρ(σ̂, σ_real)` | **> +0.20** |
| **F5-regime** | Kruskal-Wallis across `σ̂`-quintile groups | **p < 0.01** |

F5-corr's bar is deliberately 4× the directional F4 bar (+0.05): volatility
forecasting is genuinely easier than direction forecasting, and V264's evidence
is that Kronos already learned our volatility during fine-tuning. A weak result
here is not interesting.

**No post-hoc adjustment, in either direction, for any reason.** This is the
same lock V264 §4a honoured when it declined to move a bar by 0.0035.

**Verdict rule, declared in advance:**

- **all three PASS** → PROCEED; V265-2 pre-registered as a drop-only sizing
  overlay (never increases size beyond existing caps). Not built in this task.
- **any FAIL** → STOP. Kronos fully done; Track H closes for distributional use
  as well as directional.
- **2/3 PASS** → CAVEATED, with an explicit judgement call on whether the
  limited lift is worth the inference cost.

## 5. Secondary diagnostics (reported, NOT gated)

Declared here so they cannot be mistaken for post-hoc rescues:

1. **Scale-calibrated RMSE ratio.** A single in-sample global scalar
   `median(σ_real)/median(σ̂)` applied to `σ̂` before RMSE. Kronos-spread is a
   dispersion statistic and V263 diagnosed the model as under-dispersed; this
   separates *"carries no information"* from *"carries information in the wrong
   units."* It is in-sample and therefore optimistic — it can inform the
   diagnosis, never the verdict.
2. **Naive-baseline Spearman and naive-baseline KW.** Without these, an F5-corr
   or F5-regime pass could not be distinguished from "any vol proxy works here."
3. **Terminal-only `σ̂`** (dispersion at `t = H` alone, `/sqrt(H)`).

## 6. Method

- Arm: **fine-tuned** Kronos from V264 — fine-tuned tokenizer + the epoch-1
  best-by-val predictor checkpoint.
- Cells: the eight pre-declared V263/V264 cells, unchanged
  (BTC h1/h4/h12/h24, SOL h1/h24, XRP h1/h24).
- Windows: 405/cell, lookback 400, holdout fence `2025-01-01` applied to the
  **lookback start** so no scored window touches a training bar.
- `sample_count = 16`, batch 4 (= 64 concurrent sequences; V264's OOM incident
  established 64 as the stable ceiling on this host).
- Bootstrap: 10k paired resamples within cell for CI95 on all three statistics.
- Determinism: per-batch seeds are a deterministic function of the window index;
  byte-identical re-run verified before the full run.
