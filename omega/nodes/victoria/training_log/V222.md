## Hypothesis

The strategic audit's central finding (`STRATEGIC_AUDIT_2026-06.md` §1.2): the
conviction measure is a **flat unweighted composite** of one verified-alpha
signal (`sma_crossover`) averaged 1:1 with two research-flagged dead-weight
signals (`fear_greed_signal`, `ollivier_ricci_signal`), because

1. `_signal_ics` is empty — `update_signal_ics` has **zero callers** in the
   training path (V218.B probe: `IC-WEIGHTING INERT` at every startup), and
2. `_compute_weighted_conviction` early-returns the raw composite at
   `strategy.py:1032` whenever `_signal_ics` is empty.

**Bet:** with the IC subsystem actually running — pooled + per-regime seed ICs
loaded at startup, the early-return removed, per-regime weighting declared and
ON — the conviction filter down-weights the dead signals (IC 0.0 → excluded)
relative to `sma_crossover`, and **at least one gate moves > $100 from the V221
hermetic baseline**. Most likely effect: **recent goes UP** (sma_crossover gets
dominant weight where the composite was most diluted); trend may improve too.

This is a SUBSTANTIVE strategy bet (changes the conviction formula), not eval
substrate. It is measurable only because V221 made the eval hermetic ($0.00
noise floor — any nonzero Δ is real; the $100 falsifier bar is conservative).

### Cross-version vs V221-era baseline — CONTAMINATED on trend/crisis

The pre-reg required the IC-off control to reproduce V221 exactly. It does on

**Root cause — found, benign.** The `_ic_weighted_composite` fsum fence
(`signal_generation.py:246,253`) was pre-registered as "dormant this version
(stays on equal-weight fallback)." That is **wrong**: `_ic_weighted_composite`
weights from `decay_detector.ic_for(key)`, and the `SignalDecayDetector`
**self-accumulates observations during the backtest** — independent of the
seed flag. On the longer trend/crisis runs it crosses the 3-signal threshold
mid-run and takes the `math.fsum` weighted branch, which re-canonicalizes the
composite vs V221's plain `sum()` (a V211-class FP-order site). recent is short
enough that the detector never crosses the threshold → stays equal-weight →
bit-exact. Same trade counts on all three (no entry flip) confirm a pure
sizing-side re-canonicalization, exactly like the V211 basket-sort case: both
V221 (+$631.85) and V222 (−$217.71) are deterministic at $0.00 — the fence
just picked a different canonical value at a near-boundary sum. **This means
the V221-era table is invalid for cross-version trend/crisis Δ; the
within-V222 control above is the valid comparison** (and is immune — the fence
is common-mode there).

## Conclusion

**No high-water. Hypothesis REFUTED in direction — but not "IC wiring is
useless."** The pre-registered bet was "recent goes UP." Recent went **DOWN
−$2,905**. However the falsifier table's richer reading applies: the effect is
large and real (well past the $0.00 noise floor), determinism held 7/7, and
the IC weighting is cleanly **regime-conditional**:

- **trend: +$3,330.75 (clean, common-mode fence cancels) — IC HELPS.**
- **crisis: −$4,771.22 (clean) — IC HURTS.**
- **recent: −$2,905.15 — IC HURTS, because recent's profit was concentrated in
  its crisis sub-window, which IC weighting degrades.**
- selector+IC on trend: −$3,822.92, better than V221's selector-ON −$7,802.98
  by +$3,980 — IC partially rescues the (still net-negative, still worse than
  IC-on selector-OFF +$3,113) selector on trend.

Mechanism: the seed ICs are **win rates used as relative weight priors**
(documented caveat in the Seeding rationale). They over-weight `sma_crossover`
(crisis 0.52) and `ricci_curvature` (crisis 0.46) in crisis, where the
broad equal-weight composite was actually protective; and the higher
weighted-conviction inflates trade count through a filter threshold that was
never re-calibrated for the new conviction scale. The wiring is correct and
deterministic; the **values and the regime conditioning are the problem.**

Side finding (kept): the `_ic_weighted_composite` fsum fence is **load-bearing,
not dormant** — the decay detector self-activates the weighted path mid-run.
The fence is a benign FP-order correction (closes a latent V211-class channel
that was active via the decay detector on trend/crisis); the V221-era trend
baseline (+$631.85) was a pre-fence canonicalization and should not be used for
cross-version comparison.
