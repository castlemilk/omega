# Omega design and component contract

Source: [current artistic direction](https://chatgpt.com/space/page_944f9c94f3488191b65501f21dc03384), [UI atlas](https://chatgpt.com/space/page_bc328f44577081918e32b04b4daa5d61) and the imported E02/E03 feature specifications.

## Current visual direction

Original industrial precision with desert-modernist warmth: mineral black canvas, warm charcoal working surfaces, pale sand text, restrained copper/amber accents, structural rules, angular 4–6px corners and clear typography. Foundry is the dark operator treatment; Institute is the light evidence/document treatment; Deep Field is expressive orientation material. These share one component grammar.

Use cinematic architecture and terrain only in restrained onboarding or frontier illustrations. Operational tables, inputs, evidence and approval payloads stay opaque and readable. Avoid repeating fortress heroes, decorative KPI walls, fabricated live activity or glowing humanoid agents. Preserve product-art colours and originals independently of the interface theme.

The warm direction supersedes the earlier cool-accent v0.1 exploration. The earlier palette and its calculated contrasts are historical evidence, not validation of the new palette. Final runtime token values, semantic states and full contrast checks remain OME-010/014 acceptance work; this import does not invent replacement numerical tokens.

## Typography and icons

Legible humanist/grotesk sans for controls and prose; tabular numbers and restrained monospace for identifiers/telemetry; occasional editorial serif confined to orientation screens. Define size, weight and line-height for each role. Essential instructions cannot rely on tiny tracked capitals. Long names, localised amounts and 200% text zoom must remain usable.

Use an original interrupted-ring Omega mark and a coherent 24px icon grid, thin consistent strokes, slightly squared terminals and minimal detail. Proposed icon semantics: Scout beacon, Terraform terrain strata, Deploy outbound arrow, Workflows connected nodes, Agents segmented geometry. Provide monochrome/small-size/selected states and accessible names. Raster concept boards are not an implemented SVG family. No font binaries are included.

## Vocabulary

Retain **Terraform**, always explainable as **Prepare a venture**. Supporting terms: Scout, Frontiers, Validate, Deploy, Operate, Expand and Sustain. Active navigation, actions and generated copy exclude Colonise/Collanise; historical image files stay unmodified and labelled historical. Thematic wording cannot conceal actual task, permission, stage or consequence.

Omega owns operator chrome. Technical Posters, Signal Studies, Finishline Editions, Birthday Cards and later commissions retain appropriate independent product/store identities. A concept image showing a competitor as a venture is not authority to create that affiliation in production.

## Interaction and asset acceptance

Provide named icon buttons, keyboard focus, non-drag alternatives, touch-visible image controls, focus return, draft guards and reduced-motion variants. Motion must represent actual state, never imply unseen execution. Suspend decoration in background tabs and retain a stable non-WebGL path. Store source/provenance, crop, theme and decorative/product role for imagery.
