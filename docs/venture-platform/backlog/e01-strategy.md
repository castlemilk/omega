# E01 Strategy and transition

Source: https://chatgpt.com/space/page_a1f7ffdf58ac819191c05d176e490a24

Import date: 2026-11-16. Status is Proposed unless explicitly noted. Sizes express relative scope, not delivery dates. Suggested disciplines are not personal assignments. The existing digital-product pipeline is the pilot; this programme does not silently replace the current Dropshippr Workers/D1 architecture or existing project identities. It is separate from this repository's existing orchestration/infrastructure work.

## OME-001 Define the product vocabulary and venture boundary

**P0 · Product and architecture · M · Dependencies: none · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** one unambiguous vocabulary for workspace, portfolio, venture, legal entity, brand, product, channel, agent and workflow.

**Scope:** write definitions, ownership cardinalities and examples using existing poster projects; distinguish operational hierarchy from legal ownership.

**Acceptance:** each existing object maps to one defined concept or an explicit unresolved case; a venture can own multiple products and brands; a spawned venture has lineage but does not inherit credentials or budget automatically; approved vocabulary appears in navigation, API contracts and onboarding.

**Screens:** S02, S03, S06, S07.

**UI acceptance:** record the explicit Business / Product / Project / Venture mapping before writable conversion. Keep source identity and commercial ownership visible. Terraform means Prepare a venture; a draft neither activates agents nor establishes a legal entity.

**Evidence:** domain decision record and mapping fixtures. Excludes renaming or migrating production records.

## OME-002 Evaluate the Omega name and brand architecture

**P0 for public rebrand · Brand and product · M · Dependencies: OME-001 · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** choose whether Omega is the masterbrand, private codename or engine label.

**Scope:** document competing uses, search discoverability, domain candidates and relevant trademark-search leads. Compare a distinctive masterbrand plus Omega Engine with standalone Omega.

**Acceptance:** current sources and retrieval dates retained; at least three alternatives assessed against the same criteria; no domain or trademark availability asserted from a search snippet; public rename requires an explicit decision and migration checklist.

**Screens:** S01, S10, S30–S32.

**UI acceptance:** keep Omega as the working operator identity and preserve separate customer brands. Naming research and public rollout remain separate decisions.

**Evidence:** naming scorecard and decision. Excludes registrations, purchases or a legal clearance claim.

## OME-003 Map the existing Dropshippr estate and backlog

**P0 · Product and platform · M · Dependencies: OME-001 · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** preserve working products while identifying genuinely new work.

**Scope:** inventory businesses, products, saved assets, workflows, URLs, source repositories, hosted releases and outstanding issues. Reconcile OME tickets against the recorded skunkworq/dropshippr backlog once repository access is restored.

**Acceptance:** Technical Posters, Signal Studies and Finishline identities remain distinct; each existing ticket is mapped as reuse, extension, duplicate or unrelated; canonical deployed source is distinguished from prototypes; unresolved access is recorded rather than interpreted as absence.

**Screens:** S01–S32.

**UI acceptance:** trace DS01–DS64 and O01–O27 to screen families, existing OME tickets and current source contracts. Reconcile earlier Dropshippr issues before any additional tracker import; do not duplicate or close them from this mapping.

**Evidence:** traceability matrix with exact IDs and source links. Excludes closing or reopening existing issues.

## OME-004 Specify personas and measurable jobs to be done

**P1 · Product research · M · Dependencies: OME-001 · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** design around portfolio owners, makers, reviewers and operators rather than a generic chat user.

**Scope:** define triggering situations, decisions, information needs, permissions and failure costs for each role; include solo and delegated-team use.

**Acceptance:** every primary journey names a user, desired outcome and success measure; assumptions are separated from interviewed evidence; conflicting needs have a documented prioritisation; the first pilot selects one primary persona.

**Screens:** S01, S03, S17, S20, S30–S32.

**UI acceptance:** distinguish owner, creator, reviewer, operator and customer tasks. Each journey names its trigger, intended outcome, interruption recovery and next action.

**Evidence:** research protocol, interview notes where obtained and journey-to-ticket matrix. Excludes fabricated interview findings.

## OME-005 Build a source-backed competitor capability matrix

**P1 · Product research · M · Dependencies: OME-001 · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** identify differentiated behaviours worth building, not copy competitors' branding.

**Scope:** benchmark Polsia, Willo, Shopify Sidekick and an agent-orchestration platform across portfolio ownership, experiments, approvals, spend visibility, integrations and exit portability.

**Acceptance:** each capability includes a dated primary source and observed, advertised or unverified status; fees are checked directly before comparison; gaps become testable opportunities rather than claims of market exclusivity; data can be refreshed without rewriting the strategy.

**Screens:** S04, S05.

**UI acceptance:** treat competitor examples as research subjects, never as owned ventures, measured revenue or validated opportunities.

**Evidence:** comparison register and decision notes. Excludes competitor account purchases or unsupported reliability claims.

## OME-006 Capture the current operating and UX baseline

**P0 · Design and quality · M · Dependencies: none · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** quantify what the redesign must improve and preserve.

**Scope:** record supported routes, task completion, recovery behaviour, data freshness, mobile layouts, accessibility and release evidence in the current app.

**Acceptance:** separate local, hosted and physical-device observations; include create, save, uncertain-save recovery and artwork review tasks; use repeatable test fixtures; unknown measurements remain unknown rather than zero; no production screenshots are inferred from old concepts.

**Screens:** S08, S09, S12, S15, S27.

**UI acceptance:** capture existing entry-to-content and interrupted-save paths before redesign; keep prototype, local and hosted evidence separate.

**Evidence:** dated baseline and reproducible task script. Excludes repairing unrelated defects in this ticket.

## OME-007 Define the digital-product pilot and exit criteria

**P0 · Product and operations · S · Dependencies: OME-003, OME-004, OME-006 · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** prove one useful closed loop before expanding to arbitrary businesses.

**Scope:** select an existing poster product and define thesis, evidence, internal production, review packet and measurement milestones.

**Acceptance:** product identity and deliverable version are explicit; budget, review owner and stop conditions are recorded; scientific accuracy and print readiness remain separately assessed; external launch requires its own approval; failure and cancellation paths are included.

**Screens:** S03 → S08 → S09 → S12 → S11 → S15.

**UI acceptance:** prove one supervised digital-product review loop with exact artwork and Copy versions. Publication remains a subsequent capability with separate acceptance.

**Evidence:** pilot charter and pass/fail rubric. Excludes blanket permission for ads, publication, purchases or customer messaging.

## OME-008 Establish programme sequencing and decision ownership

**P1 · Product and delivery · S · Dependencies: OME-003, OME-007 · Status: Proposed**

**Outcome:** keep a large backlog executable rather than treating all tickets as the next sprint.

**Scope:** assign gates A–E, dependency links, responsible disciplines, decision records and evidence requirements; set limits on concurrent work.

**Acceptance:** no dependency cycles; proposed work is distinguished from ready work; blocked reasons name the missing decision or capability; changes to scope or sequencing are logged; completion requires acceptance evidence and does not imply deployment.

**Screens:** all screen families.

**UI acceptance:** deliver foundations, read-only scope, content/review, formation, controlled runtime and connected commerce in dependency order. Physical operations and custom-logo commissions remain deferred.

**Evidence:** ordered pilot queue and dependency audit. Excludes invented dates, personal assignments or automatic scheduling.
