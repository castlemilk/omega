## What Is It?

The tweet promotes a 361-page PDF of **"151 Trading Strategies"** — a comprehensive reference cataloguing 150+ quantitative trading strategies across all major asset classes. Written by Zura Kakushadze (ex-WorldQuant Managing Director, Cornell PhD in theoretical physics) and Juan Andrés Serur.

**Key stats:**
- 550+ mathematical formulas with full derivations
- 2,000+ bibliographic references
- 900+ glossary/acronym definitions
- Source code for out-of-sample backtesting
- Some strategies use ML (neural nets, Bayes, KNN)
- Available on [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3247865) and [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04492)

**⚠️ Retraction notice:** Springer has retracted the book from their platform. The retraction reason is not publicly documented in search results. The content remains available on SSRN and arXiv. Treat formulas as reference material requiring independent validation, not as peer-reviewed results.

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. Funding Rate Carry Harvesting (Priority: P0)

**What:** Systematically harvest crypto funding rate carry. When perpetual funding rates are elevated (>0.03% per 8h), go long spot + short perp to collect the funding premium while being delta-neutral.

**Why it fits:** This is the crypto equivalent of the FX carry trade — one of the most durable edges in traditional finance. Kakushadze documents it formally. Victoria already reads funding rates in `derivatives_signals.py` but doesn't act on them as a standalone strategy.

**Feasibility:** ★★★★★ — Data already flows in. Implementation is a new strategy mode in `strategy.py`.
**Expected Alpha:** ★★★★★ — Funding rate carry has historically generated 20-40% APY in crypto with minimal drawdown when properly managed.
**Effort:** Low — 200-400 lines. Add a `FundingCarryStrategy` that triggers when funding > threshold AND regime is not crisis.

### 2. Cointegration-Based Pairs Trading (Priority: P0)

**What:** Formal Engle-Granger or Johansen cointegration testing on crypto pairs. When the spread deviates >2σ from equilibrium, mean-revert. Key pairs: ETH/BTC, SOL/ETH, MATIC/SOL.

**Why it fits:** Victoria has correlation analysis but no formal cointegration framework. Crypto pairs exhibit strong cointegration in ranging regimes (exactly when momentum signals are weakest). This is complementary — it generates alpha precisely when our momentum signals go flat.

**Feasibility:** ★★★★ — `statsmodels` has Engle-Granger and Johansen built-in.
**Expected Alpha:** ★★★★ — Crypto pairs dislocate frequently and violently, creating wide spreads that mean-revert.
**Effort:** Medium — 500-800 lines. New `PairsSignal` class + integration with regime detector (only trade in sideways regime).

### 3. Cross-Sectional Momentum Factor (Priority: P1)

**What:** Formal cross-sectional momentum: rank our crypto universe by trailing returns, go long top quintile, short bottom quintile. Jegadeesh-Titman (1993) applied to crypto.

**Why it fits:** We have per-asset momentum but no cross-sectional ranking. The book formalizes the approach with proper portfolio construction and rebalancing rules.

**Feasibility:** ★★★★ — We already have the data and ranking infrastructure.
**Expected Alpha:** ★★★ — Cross-sectional momentum works in crypto but has higher drawdowns than in equities due to correlation spikes.
**Effort:** Low — 300-500 lines. Add `CrossSectionalMomentum` to `signals_advanced.py`.

## Overall Verdict

**Rating: Useful reference, not a game-changer. Medium priority.**

The "151 Trading Strategies" book is an **encyclopedia**, not a research breakthrough. It catalogues well-known strategies with mathematical rigor, which makes it a good reference for ensuring we haven't missed obvious strategy families. The @quantscience_ framing of "unlocks every algorithm used by hedge funds" is marketing hyperbole — these are textbook strategies, not proprietary hedge fund alpha.

**What we should extract:**
1. Funding rate carry (immediate, high conviction)
2. Cointegration pairs trading (next sprint, fills a gap in our ranging-market coverage)
3. Cross-sectional momentum factor (formalize what we partially do)

**What we should NOT do:**
- Don't try to implement all 151 strategies. Most are for asset classes we don't trade.
- Don't treat the retracted book as gospel — validate all formulas independently.
- Don't expect novel alpha from a publicly available 2018 textbook. The edge is in our execution, not in strategy selection.

**Key caveat:** The book has been **retracted by Springer** (reason undisclosed). While the mathematical content is likely sound (it's mostly well-known finance), the retraction means we should independently verify any formula before production use.

**Net assessment for Victoria:** +0.5% to +2% improvement in risk-adjusted returns if we implement the top 3 recommendations, primarily from funding carry (new alpha source) and pairs trading (fills ranging-market gap). This is incremental, not transformational.
