## Heterogeneous Exposures to Systematic and Idiosyncratic Risk across Crypto Assets (arXiv 2506.21100)
**Source:** adjacent to @browomo / @0xricker — https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21100
**Type:** paper
**Score:** 3/5 × 3/5 = 9/25 — Watch

**Summary:** Two-stage divide-and-conquer: stage 1 fits asset-level IV regressions for idiosyncratic vs market exposure; stage 2 PCA on residuals + high-dim variable selection maps latent factors to macro-financial uncertainty. Covers >80% of crypto market cap. Finds Green/DeFi assets carry higher economy-wide risk; stablecoins much lower; short-term mean reversion present.

**Gap analysis:**
- Does Omega do this? Partial — Omega has a PCA regime signal + HMM, but no asset-class-level idiosyncratic-vs-systematic decomposition or mapping to macro uncertainty residuals.
- What would change: a new `risk_decomposition` node under `omega/nodes/victoria/` that runs the two-stage IV+PCA on the crypto basket; feeds an asset-class risk feature into the ensemble.
- Dependencies: macro uncertainty series (VIX, EPU, MOVE) — Omega currently has none of these wired; would need a fetcher.

**Recommendation:** Skip for now. The macro-data dependency (VIX/EPU/MOVE) is a non-trivial new ingestion path, and the result (asset-class risk tilt) duplicates much of what `bayesian_regime.py` + `confidence_surface.py` already deliver. Revisit if/when Omega adds a macro layer.

## Quantifying Crypto Portfolio Risk: Simulation-Based Framework (arXiv 2507.08915)
**Source:** adjacent to @data_sn13 / @0xricker — https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08915
**Type:** paper
**Score:** 3/5 × 4/5 = 12/25 — Queue

**Summary:** Modular MC framework combining (1) volatility stress tests, (2) stablecoin hedging legs, (3) correlation-based contagion propagation, (4) mean-variance optimization. Validated 2020–2024 on BTC/ETH/USDT. Acknowledges fat tails / non-normality that traditional models miss.

**Gap analysis:**
- Does Omega do this? No — Omega has ATR, vol-regime, and a drawdown gate, but no Monte Carlo path simulation, no contagion correlation matrix, no stablecoin hedge sizing.
- What would change: a new pre-trade `portfolio_risk` checkpoint that runs N MC paths conditional on current regime/vol; could gate position sizing in `continuous_sizing` (V148 best-of-phases).
- Dependencies: numpy/scipy already present; needs a correlation estimator across the basket and a vol model (GARCH or realized vol — Omega has realized vol via ATR/HMM).

**Recommendation:** Queue. Worth a small spike: add an `omega/nodes/victoria/mc_risk_check.py` that runs ~1000 paths over the next K cycles using current regime-conditional vol + pairwise correlation, returning a max-drawdown distribution. Wire into `exit_controller.py` to scale down sizing when 95th-percentile DD exceeds threshold. Low infra cost, directly addresses V148's continuous-sizing risk surface.
