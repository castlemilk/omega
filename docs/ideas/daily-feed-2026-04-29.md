## Microstructural Dynamics in Cryptocurrency Limit Order Books (arXiv 2506.05764)
**Source:** arXiv — https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05764
**Type:** paper
**Score:** 4/5 × 2/5 = 8/25 — Watch

**Summary:** Benchmarks logistic regression, XGBoost, DeepLOB, and Conv1D+LSTM on BTC/USDT Bybit LOB data with two preprocessing pipelines (Kalman, Savitzky-Golay). Headline finding: with proper noise filtering, simpler models match or exceed deep architectures, and inference is meaningfully faster. Provides a microstructure feature-engineering recipe more than a new model.

**Gap analysis:**
- Does Omega do this? No — Omega has no L2/order-book ingestion (known gap in `project_omega.md`). All current signals are bar-level (SMA/RSI/MACD/BB/ATR/OBV/funding/OI/etc.).
- What would change: Would require a new ingest path (LOB snapshots + delta stream) and a new microstructure signal node. The Kalman/Savitzky-Golay preprocessing could later live as utilities in `omega/nodes/victoria/`.
- Dependencies: Bybit (or Coinbase L2 since Bybit is geo-blocked from US per `reference_exchange_apis.md`) WebSocket client, persistent LOB store, ~100ms cadence loop — fundamentally incompatible with current polling architecture.

**Recommendation:** Watch, don't queue. The signal class is real and Omega has a known L2 gap, but the dependency chain (streaming infra, US-friendly L2 source, sub-second cadence) is a multi-week build and conflicts with Omega's "all polling" baseline. Revisit only when/if a streaming layer is on the roadmap. If it ever lands, the Savitzky-Golay preprocessing trick is the cheap part to lift first — drop it as a feature transform on whatever LOB features are computed.

**Summary:** Roundup of on-chain signals analysts are using for 2026 cycle calls. Highlights two specific composites: (1) **stablecoin velocity** = stablecoin transaction volume / stablecoin market cap, framed as the cleanest proxy for "real" on-chain activity, with stablecoin supply growth claimed at ~0.87 correlation with BTC and often leading rallies; (2) **on-chain open interest / total decentralized volume** as an early-warning threshold for systemic leverage risk.

**Gap analysis:**
- Does Omega do this? Partial. Omega has funding rate, open interest, and DeFi TVL signals, but no stablecoin velocity and no DEX-OI-to-DEX-volume composite. Existing OI signal is exchange-level (CEX), not on-chain.
- What would change: Two new signals in the Victoria signal layer. Stablecoin velocity is a single ratio fed by Glassnode/Dune/CoinMetrics free endpoints. On-chain OI/DEX-vol ratio is also a ratio but needs DEX OI (Hyperliquid, dYdX, Drift) plus DEX volume (DefiLlama already in stack).
- Dependencies: Stablecoin supply + transfer volume series (Glassnode `entities` series or Dune queries — both exist as free tiers); DEX OI source (Hyperliquid public stats API, no auth) for the second composite. No new infrastructure — fits existing polling cadence and the Python signal node pattern.

**Recommendation:** Queue. Add `stablecoin_velocity` as a regime-flavoured signal in `omega/nodes/victoria/signal_generation.py` alongside the existing TVL/funding/OI signals — should slot in with no orchestrator changes. The 0.87-correlation claim is suspect (likely overfit / coincident, not leading) so the implementation must include the standard `tests/test_action_contracts.py`-style contract test plus a Brier calibration check before being weight-promoted by the meta-learner. Treat the DEX-OI/DEX-vol ratio as a separate follow-up — useful as a regime modifier (high values → de-risk longs) more than a directional signal. Both are cheap: one polling endpoint each, no new deps.
